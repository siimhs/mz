# Sweep 8976118 … 9942556 (topics 2011-02 … 2012-04)

2,732 topics, 2,658 of them not in `covered-topics.json`. I scanned every title. Most are
off-topic (cups, zones, finance, youth admin). Round 1 did not cover the
**post-S40 era**, which runs from the v1.98 sim (live 2011-10-18) to the v2.18 beta and release
(2012-03 … 04), so I focused on it.

Sim eras used below:
- **old** = before 2011-10-18 (pre-S40)
- **S40** = sim v1.98 (2011-10-18 … ~2012-04-16)
- **v2.18** = beta from 2012-03-08, live ~2012-04-17 [36596066 nikolazpsadmin: release
  postponed "until at least 16 April"; 36652728, 2012-04-17: "the new sim looks better"]

All of these are older than today's engine. v2.18 is the newest engine in this range.

## Summary
- **The best passing style changes with each sim.** Old: short passing. S40: Wings is best and long ball is "a non starter"
  [36214857 frankzappa, 36151673 heddo, 35629685 kkostov] (consensus). v2.18: per the news,
  "wing play has become a lot less effective and now is considered the weakest playing style"
  [36436677 nikolazpsadmin, quoting the news] (official via poster). Short passing dominates, played
  "ultra narrow" [36660752 prednibe, 36719579].
- **Under Wings in v2.18, Aerial Passing decides pass success for everyone, not only wingers.** Players "use aerial passes
  all the time even in midfield". The 9-AP player is the best passer [36551377, 36553874 mosmosmos]
  (consensus, 2 posters).
- **The choice of passing style should follow the team's average Passing vs Aerial Passing** [36219938 xellu (FA)] (opinion, FA).
  This supports a model where the tactic and the skills interact.
- **Passing skill matters more in v2.18** ("passing skill is back") [36472907 chelseax1, 36652728] (consensus).
- **Speed is still the gate skill.** England U21 NT minimum (2012-02): "speed above 8 and stamina above 6",
  GK "10 in keeping" [36239832 jamie1976]. In v2.18, mosmosmos says Speed is "so incredibly over weighted" [36705019,
  36710401, 36718322]. Others say Stamina is the key [36707294]. Contested, but nobody here says Speed is weak.
- **Keepers got much stronger in S40.** In the Estonian top league, the share of on-target shots scored fell from 35% to 13%, and of all shots from 20% to 8–9%
  [35382022, 35567881 capzzz]. v2.18 posters still find keepers "too strong" [36636781, 36498617]. This supports a high Keeping weight.
- **GK Ball Control** helps catch the ball instead of parrying it, which means fewer rebounds (consensus, 4 posters, 2011-07 … 2012-03). The order is still
  Keeping, Stamina, then PI/BC/Speed. Passing is useless for a GK (ground passes).
- **PI and cards.** Crew says PI matters for cards (per posters) [35754012]. "2 TA + 5 PI better in a defensive
  role than 5 TA + 2 PI" [36220203 frankzappa] (opinion). In v2.18, PI is "ultra important for defenders, not
  for strikers" [36624181] (opinion).
- **Experience.** v2.18 beta testers perceived an *increased* Exp weight [36565666 ninja_mad, 36374734 clabbem]
  (2 posters). That reverses the S40 Crew statement (Exp reduced). It is contested and depends on the era.
- **Set Plays** has little visible effect on penalties. "0.1% effect" [36462062 aemi]. SP 7 was the worst penalty taker
  [36452089], and a taker with 0 SP and 3 SHO scored [36681775]. Contested (apwall21 sees an effect on free kicks and throw-ins).
- **Heading in S40** depends on the tactic. High wingers produce many headed goals, "7 of 10 goals by header" [35427836]
  vs [35441556, 35442048]. Crew: heading was unchanged in v2.18 [darkline quoting Sim notes, 36366649].
  For strikers, train it after Spd/Sta/BC/SHO/PI, and it matters under Wings [36020859, 36028072].
- **Balance beats peaks.** "8 SH + 8 BC does better than 10 SH + 6 BC" [36380812]. "Slightly lower primaries with
  a good set of secondaries any day" [35491358 mosmosmos]. Against this, one pre-S40 claim says returns *increase* from the 7th ball [33915911]
  (opinion, old sim). Contested.
- **Wide full-backs sit idle.** "Forget attacking full backs". Put fast defenders at CB to run down strikers
  [35429949, 35398527, 35646527] (consensus, S40).
- **Better teams win more often** in the H2H/S40 sim [34710998 patrick7777, Crew] and in v2.18 [36504829, 36498993].
  Some believe team *averages* matter, so a weak player overperforms in a strong team [36720446 civilu] (opinion).

## Findings

### Crew / official
- patrick7777 (2011-08-04): the new (H2H) sim "is focussing on football, better tactics combined with the better
  players will win more games". In >100 dev test matches the stronger team never lost [34710998].
- patrick7777 (2012-04-25): no updates since v2.18 was released; "No news means no change" [36710791].
- Sim notes v2.18 (quoted by posters): heading was "not changed for V2.18, we will look again" [36366649, 36365161].
  Wing play is now the weakest style [36436677].
- Short-handed play: a team that starts with fewer than 11 players is punished; a red card gives no boost or punishment (Crew, per posters)
  [35993976, 35993434].

### Skills
- Spd/Sta/BC as the "base" for every outfield position: consensus 2011–2012 [35325246, 35365586, 34013684,
  36156894, 35673811 darkline picks 10 Spd + 10 Sta]. BC 5 is too low for a defender [35035959].
- S40 "pinball" one-touch passing: Speed and Stamina show less on the ball [35324106, 35360300]. mosmosmos: BC "HUGE" for
  strikers, Speed matters off the ball for mids [35352066]. Contested.
- v2.18 stamina: 6–7 Sta players fade in the second half [36369459] (opinion).
- Tackling for strikers is "in" in v2.18 (a striker with 3 Tac steals the ball) [36472907]. In S40 it was "not that useful" [36020859].

### GK
- Keeping, then Sta, PI, Spd, BC [35362488]; Sta, PI, BC, Spd [36329592]; BC as the "major secondary" [34533604];
  BC limits rebounds (2 → 4 → 6 anecdote) [36305886]; PI for positioning [36305886]; Sta ≥ 4 [36283612].
- GK Set Plays is used in penalty shoot-outs [36347004] (opinion).

### Tactics / formation
- One striker only works with attacking mids [34518601] (opinion, old). A supporting striker scores the most [36592487] (opinion).
- Committed pressing raises cards; use it only with high-Sta players [36056768] (consensus with the manual).

## Changes to RESULTS.md
1. **Make passing-style modifiers era-aware and stronger on AP under Wings.** Add AP×1.3 for CM and CD under
   Wings (v2.18: midfield also passes in the air under Wings) [36551377, 36553874]. Note that the best style
   changed with each sim (S40 Wings, v2.18 Short) [36214857, 36436677]. Do not hard-code which style is "best".
2. **Long balls: lower the AP boosts** (CD AP×1.6 → ×1.3, GK ×1.5 → ×1.3). Long ball was "a non starter" in S40
   [36214857], and in v2.18 long balls "go directly to the player or the opposition" [36365161]. This fits the round-1
   finding that 2.5 AP was enough.
3. **Speed: do not lower it.** Keep ST/W SPD at .17/.20, and consider raising CD SPD .17 → .19. Speed is the NT gate skill
   (>8) [36239832], v2.18 "over weighted" [36705019], and fast defenders belong at CB [35429949]. This partly answers the
   open question on defender Speed after S40.
4. **GK: BC .10 → .13, AP .05 → .03, SP .02 → .03** (shoot-outs). Take the difference from STA (.10 → .08). BC
   limits rebounds [36283612, 36305886, 34533604, 36329592]. Passing (ground) stays at 0 [35361682]. Keeping
   stays dominant: post-S40 keepers convert far fewer shots into goals against them [35382022].
5. **CD PI .15 → .17, ST PI .15 → .12** (opinion level, era v2.18) [36624181, 36220203, 35034244]. The extra PI weight
   also stands for the card risk that PI reduces.
6. **Experience E: keep it small but flag it as era-dependent.** S40 Crew said Exp was reduced, while v2.18 testers felt it was raised
   [36565666, 36374734]. Add this to "Contradictions left open".
7. **setPieceScore: lower SP 0.5 → 0.35, add PI 0.15** (0.35 SP + 0.3 SHO + 0.2 AP + 0.15 PI). Evidence that SP
   matters for penalties is weak or negative [36462062, 36452089, 36681775]. Low priority.
8. **shape(): keep it concave.** Post-S40 evidence favours balance [36380812, 35491358]. Record the pre-S40
   "convex from the 7th ball" claim [33915911] as contested.
9. **FB:** confirms that wide full-backs are idle in narrow or short styles. Under Short (v2.18 ultra narrow), add FB SPD×0.9.
10. **Team effect:** posters believe team averages lift weak players [36720446]. This matches the Crew team-effect fact.
    It stays out of scope, but supports a later team-level aggregate.
