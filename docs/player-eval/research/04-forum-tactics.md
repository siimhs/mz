# Tactics vs skill weights (forum DB + manual A04)

Scope: how passing style, mentality, pressing, formation and set-piece roles
change which skills matter, by position. Sources: local forum DB (forum 10,
2011-02 … 2012-02; it **does** include ~4 months after the new sim went live
on 2011-10-18) and manual A04/A05.

Who is "official" here: `patrick7777` is the Power Challenge CEO and the sim
developer voice; his posts carry the "(Crew) (GPA)" tag (quoted in
[post 35172210, 2011-10-02, pandish]) even though the DB has no role for him.
Users with DB role "Crew" (mikelony, blackwater, marcus, thunderexpress,
powdersnow) posted almost nothing about the sim; mikelony's one test report is
listed below. `dagriggstar` is "RA" (a regional admin, not sim staff), so his
posts count as opinion.

Timeline: "pre-S40" = old sim (before 2011-10-18). "H2H/test" = the new sim
while it was being tested (H2H V1.x, Jul–Oct 2011). "post-S40" = live new sim
(V2.0x, from 2011-10-18) [post 35303637, 2011-10-18, patrick7777].

Skill name aliases that posters use: "crossing", "crossballs", "CB",
"high passing", "long ball skill" all mean **Aerial Passing (AP)**. The skill
was renamed but its mechanics did not change [post 35628640, 2011-12-03,
wanttostrokemypuppy].

## Summary (the answer)

1. **The passing styles are Short passing, Long balls and Wings.** There is no
   separate "counter attack" option [manual A04 Tactics]. **official**
2. **The user's hypothesis is mostly right, with two changes.** Long balls do
   use AP (passes in the air), and short passing keeps the ball on the ground.
   But in the 2011 sim, Long ball was the weakest and least-developed style.
   AP gained most of its weight through **Wings** (wingers' crosses), not
   through Long ball. **consensus + official**
3. **Under Long ball, AP should go up for defenders and the keeper (the ones
   who hit the long ball), and midfielders' passing should go down, because
   the ball skips the midfield.** Strikers need ball control and speed to
   receive. For defenders, AP still ranks below tackling, speed and stamina.
   **opinion/consensus, mostly pre-S40**
4. **Under Wings, the winger is the key player.** Patrick lists the winger
   skills as pace, stamina, ball control and AP/passing, and says positioning
   (very wide) matters. Strikers' **heading** goes up because "heading is a
   useful skill for strikers, particularly in teams playing wings, where the
   wingers have good aerial passing" [post 35359536]. **official**
5. **Heading is tied to aerial play.** In the new sim, "Air passing and heading
   are now far more important skills than before" [post 34779168, 2011-08-12,
   patrick7777]. **official** Whether this shows up in live matches is
   **contested**: posters' tests with 0/3/7 heading strikers showed no
   difference, and crosses seemed to land well whatever the winger's AP.
6. **Defenders hardly head the ball away in the live new sim.** Patrick lists
   it as a planned patch: "We wanted defender to have a clearer opportunity to
   head away crossed balls" [post 35567056]. So defender heading is low value
   for now. **official**
7. **Short passing** is about ground passing, passing accuracy and PI. Post-S40
   posters called it the worst style; pre-S40 it was the dominant one.
   **consensus**
8. **Mentality changes the risks defenders take, not skill use directly.**
   Attacking means defenders take more risks; Defensive means fewer.
   [post 35252335, 2011-10-12, patrick7777]. **official** There is no evidence
   that mentality changes skill weights.
9. **Pressing: Committed means more harassing, more stamina used and more
   fouls; Passive means the reverse** [manual A04]. So Committed raises the
   weight of **stamina** (all outfield players), and PI and experience matter
   more because they cut cards [post 35194786, 35367521]. **official**
10. **Speed and stamina lost weight in the new sim.** They were the
    first-trained skills pre-S40 (consensus). Post-S40, several posters say
    their importance dropped, while passing, heading, AP and PI rose.
    **consensus (post-S40), not confirmed by staff.**
11. **Formation matters more for which positions are actually involved than
    for skill weights.** In 3-back or narrow setups the wide defenders often
    do nothing and the central defenders do all the work. Patrick says
    traditional formations (4-4-2, wide wingers) "naturally play better".
    **official + consensus**
12. **Set pieces:** corners, long free kicks and goal kicks behave like aerial
    passes, so the taker needs **AP**. The Set Plays skill seems to boost only
    direct free-kick shots and penalties, and it is a low priority for most
    managers. **consensus (pre- and post-S40), no official statement**
13. **Keepers:** their distribution is all aerial (AP plus set plays), and
    passing is useless for them [post 35208087]. **opinion** Keepers need
    stamina [post 35598946, patrick7777]. **official**
14. **Per-ball differences between skill levels felt small in the new sim**
    (3 vs 10 balls), so tactic modifiers should be modest multipliers, not big
    swings. **consensus complaint** Patrick says the per-stat influence was
    still being rebalanced [post 35248205].

## Findings

### 1. Passing styles

**Names and intent.** Short passing is patient and gives possession. Long
balls go up to the forwards: quick, but "less precise passes". Wings
"channel their attacks down the Wings … with crosses to your strikers"
[manual A04 Tactics]. **official**

**Which skill each style uses** (post-S40):
- "short passing is basically keeping the ball on the ground. Wings is a
  mixture of ground ball and aerial passing. Long balls is aerial passing."
  [post 35354025, 2011-10-24, safieeamat]. **opinion**, agrees with the AP
  definition: "finding team-mates with long, aerial balls from anywhere on the
  pitch" [manual A05 Training]. **official**
- "All long ball passes seemingly have to be in the air and then immediately
  met with a header" [post 35294790, 2011-10-17, adv41449]. **opinion**
- AP quality changes how well the ball is received: from a poor long-ball
  passer "the receiver has problems controlling the ball"; from good passers
  receivers "usually take a quick shot or make a quick pass"
  [post 35533817, 2011-11-18, darkline]. **opinion**. It also changes the
  defence: a high-AP pass lets the winger take the ball cleanly, while a low-AP
  pass lets the defender "nick the ball" [post 35577841, 2011-11-24,
  mosmosmos]. **opinion**
- The opposite view: "players with an average of 2,5 balls at long balls, and
  90% of their long balls reach" [post 35408812, 2011-10-31, konstas]. Teams
  with no AP still complete long passes. **contested**
- "Team has no aerial passing for the Long passing tactic, it would play worse
  using anything other than Short passing" [post 35450083, 2011-11-06,
  overthetop3]. **opinion**. This supports matching the style to the squad's
  AP.

**How strong each style is.**
- H2H 1.34: "longball is the 'weakest' style", but picking a style to suit
  your squad gives "significantly better results" [post 34816513, 2011-08-16,
  patrick7777]. **official**
- V2.02 (the release version): "whilst wings are better in V2.02 (vs live)
  short pass and long pass can beat wings … it depends on the type of players
  you have" [post 35299246, 2011-10-17, patrick7777]. **official**
- Post-S40 posters: "passing tactics is just suicide … long balls seems
  somewhere between pass and wings" [post 35255381, 2011-10-12, civilu].
  "short passes dont work well … Long balls … dont seem to do anything
  specificly useful. So that leave us wings" [post 35408812, konstas].
  "long ball is just short passing with more short passing … I'm not even sure
  it's been touched" [post 35455768, 2011-11-06, dagriggstar]. **consensus:
  Wings > Long ≈ Short (post-S40).**
- Pre-S40, the reverse: "In previous sim there was only one working tactic and
  that was short pass … through the middle" [post 35367120, 2011-10-26,
  prednibe]. "wings aren't effective if you're playing 'Use wings'"
  [post 33887996, 2011-05-05, matttombstone]. **consensus (pre-S40)**

**Wings: skills by position.**
- Wingers: "the kind of skills a 'Winger' would have, pace, stamina, ball
  control, aerial passing/passing, these skills will also affect how well a
  player and team can play with Wings" [post 34451557, 2011-07-08,
  patrick7777]. **official**. He also criticised one manager's wingers for
  "very poor passing and high passing [=AP] skills" [post 35196036,
  2011-10-05]. **official**
- The wing tactic makes wingers run with the ball and cross, on the ground or
  in the air, even to marked team-mates [post 34779168, 2011-08-12]. At V2.02:
  "Winger will cross if they can … use ground passes … cut inside and shoot"
  [post 35299246]. So for wingers, ball control and speed (running) plus AP
  (crosses) plus passing (ground crosses) all count. **official**
- Wingers must be placed very wide, near the touchline, to "feel like a
  Winger" [post 34451557; 35159177; 35194786, patrick7777]. **official**.
  Positioning, not only skills, decides whether the winger multiplier applies.
- "wings on the midfield are superior to wings in the attack. A wing player is
  the only player on the pitch that will run with the ball" [post 35367120,
  prednibe]. **opinion**
- Strikers under Wings: heading goes up (see section 2). One poster had a
  "4-3-3 wide with a very good central forward (at least 6 ball on headers).
  From 20 shots 18 are headers" [post 35337362, 2011-10-22, xzq]. **opinion**
- Defenders facing Wings: fullbacks must stop wingers who run with the ball.
  Defenders "usualy run behind the running winger" [post 35408812, konstas].
  That points to speed for fullbacks. **opinion**

**Long ball: skills by position.**
- Defenders: "aerial pass is hugely important to defenders if you play long
  ball. Problem is tackling, speed and stamina always come before that"
  [post 33883982, 2011-05-04, alx460]. A second poster agreed
  [post 33885838, ade-pm]. **consensus (pre-S40)**
- Strikers: "Got to have fast strikers if you are playing long balls!"
  [post 34772326, 2011-08-11, willo1904]. **opinion (pre-S40)**
- Midfielders are bypassed: "since i play with consecutive long balls it
  wouldnt make any real difference as middlefielder either"
  [post 35410979, 2011-10-31, konstas]. **opinion**
- "defenders head with long ball and wings" [post 35540436, 2011-11-19,
  steviedee]. This conflicts with the claim that defenders never head
  [post 35362346, jamie1976]. **contested**
- Long balls produced many long-range shots [post 35343400, 2011-10-23,
  sephiroth]. **opinion**. Long shots point to shooting for midfielders and
  strikers.
- Long ball "encourages a direct simplistic style of play"
  [post 35131853, 2011-09-27, patrick7777]. **official**

**Short passing: skills by position.**
- Pre-S40 wingers in a short-passing tactic "utilise more passing than
  crossing" [post 33887996, matttombstone]. **opinion**
- Short passing needs "passing and PI type skills"; the other two styles
  "really emphasize speed and stamina" [post 35269799, 2011-10-14,
  dagriggstar]. **opinion (test sim)**
- Post-S40, poor decisions under short passing (backpasses, passing for no
  reason) lowered its value [post 35408812]. **consensus**. Patrick:
  "Passing is better" in the new sim [post 35124101], and V1.2 "improved the
  accuracy of passing, based on the passing ability of the player"
  [post 34449451]. **official**

### 2. Heading and aerial play

- V1.2: "Players will head the ball more accurately, based on their heading
  ability … Players are better able to control air passes" [post 34449451,
  2011-07-08, patrick7777]. **official**
- V1.34: "Air passing and heading are now far more important skills than
  before" [post 34779168, 2011-08-12, patrick7777]. **official**
- Players head more often, "particularly matches where players lack the
  ability to pass along the ground or in the air" [post 34375990, 2011-07-01,
  patrick7777]. **official**
- Post-S40: "heading is a useful skill for strikers, particularly in teams
  playing wings, where the wingers have good aerial passing" [post 35359536,
  2011-10-25; repeated in 35364885, patrick7777]. **official**
- Contested in practice. "Heading is now a very useful skill" [post 35358249,
  xellu]. Against that, a 7-heading defender used as a striker did no better
  [post 35358753, civilu], and "No difference at all" between 7, 3 and 0
  heading [post 35362547, capcash]. capcash says crosses land perfectly
  whatever the winger's AP [post 35362551]. baltovenaar agreed
  [post 35362586], but then scored 2 headers with an 8-AP winger
  [post 35366025]. mosmosmos says the skills are used but there is not "a big
  enough difference between 3 balls and 10 balls, and that is in EVERY skill"
  [post 35368493]. **contested**
- Headers come from wingers' high crosses rather than from plain aerial
  passes [post 35369401, coldfire01]. **opinion**
- Pre-S40: "heading however is [completely useless]" [post 33886074,
  gebruikersnaam_]. **opinion (pre-S40)**
- Defenders: "defenders never head the ball" [post 35362346, 2011-10-25,
  jamie1976]; "A defensive player will never head the ball away"
  [post 35367120, prednibe]. Staff confirm this as a known issue
  [post 35567056, patrick7777]. **official + consensus**

### 3. Mentality

- Attacking: "your defenders will take more risks … more likely to concede".
  Balanced: attack, but keep possession when 2–3 goals up. Defensive: "defend
  first, attack second … less likely to concede" [post 35252335, 2011-10-12,
  patrick7777]. **official**
- Test sim 1.84 also changed tempo automatically (teams ahead keep
  possession; all teams speed up in the last ~10 minutes of each half), as
  quoted in [post 35246957, sverrerobert]. Patrick later said tempo is now
  more under the manager's control [post 35247803]. **official (version
  specific)**
- Posters saw little difference between attacking and normal, except that
  defenders were "more willing to play forward" [post 35340971, 2011-10-22,
  fathertaylor]. **opinion**
- No source links mentality to a specific skill. A reasonable guess: Attacking
  raises the value of defenders' speed and tackling (recovering from risks)
  and of midfielders' and strikers' attacking skills. This is
  **inference, not sourced**.

### 4. Pressing

- Committed means more harassment of the ball carrier, more stamina used and
  a higher foul risk. Passive saves stamina and cuts cards [manual A04].
  **official**
- Passive "makes your team play to a lower standard … For a low level stamina
  team, Passive can be a viable option" [post 36213260, 2012-02-16,
  matttombstone]. **opinion**
- "Dont use commited, your players cant take it" (advice to a weaker team)
  [post 35551077, 2011-11-21, bestplaymaker2134]. Committed also brings
  injuries [post 33891705/33892395, 2011-05, jamie1976]. **consensus**
- "i tried passive pressing once - don't do it. I lost 5-0" [post 35339989,
  sephiroth]. Several post-S40 managers who reported success used
  wings/attacking/committed [post 35347292, 35384478, 35405495]. **opinion**
- Cards: "better players (faster, better PI) are able to make more tackles … more chances for a … card" [post 35172210, patrick7777]. "Their intelligence and experience will allow them to avoid making tackles that would have resulted in a card" [post 35194786, patrick7777]. Experience is used for card avoidance [post 35367521]. **official**. So Committed pressing makes PI and experience worth more for defenders and defensive midfielders.
- Pre-S40: "Your defenders usually go out of position if you play aggressive"
  [post 33748924, 2011-04-21, darkline]. **opinion**
- Stamina mechanics: stamina may depend on distance run [post 35597963,
  darkline, recalling an old crew statement]; "Keepers need stamina … The
  lower their stamina, the greater the risk they will become tired"
  [post 35598946, patrick7777]. **official for keepers**

### 5. Formation

- "We do not 'punish' or 'reward' a particular line up, but the players
  naturally play better in traditional formations" (e.g. 4-4-2)
  [post 34816513, patrick7777]. **official**. Post-S40 posters found 4-4-2
  strong [post 35337484, ade-pm; 35339989, sephiroth; 35383165]. **consensus**
- Wide defenders are often idle. In the test sim, "Side defenders are not
  playing … Only central defenders participate" [post 35139156, 2011-09-28,
  mikelony (Crew role)]. "With three defenders the outside defenders do little
  to nothing, the centre defender is the one doing all the dirty work"
  [post 35135161, baltovenaar]. Against a narrow mirror 4-3-3, "the side
  defenders … make 0 tackle attempts" [post 35331798, 2011-10-21, jauho].
  V1.63 changed defender AI so that fullbacks support the CBs
  [post 35157183, patrick7777]. After that, "full backs are actually active"
  [post 35283662, 2011-10-16, mosmosmos]. **contested/version dependent**.
  Takeaway: weight central defenders above wide ones, unless the opponent
  plays wingers.
- New-sim defenders "stay in position and doesn't give chase as often", so
  "2 central Ds and then 3 guys in front" works. "Ds need PI and experience to
  avoid cards and penalties" [post 35367120, prednibe]. **opinion**. This
  contradicts "3 defenders need more speed": there is no source saying fewer
  defenders need more speed. The evidence points to positional coverage
  instead.
- A single striker has no upside in the new sim [post 35367120]. A lone
  striker needs to be good [post 35331937, mattsadler]. **opinion**
- Formations interact with Wings. A 5-2-3 "was effective at nullifying your
  wingers", and its 2-man midfield was then vulnerable to a 3-man midfield
  [post 35196036, patrick7777]. **official**

### 6. Set plays and set-piece takers

- Manual: "Teams containing a player with good skill ratings in this
  attribute will be more effective from corners, free kicks and penalties"
  [manual A05]. Takers are set under Roles [manual A04]. **official**
- Forum: "Goal Kick, Corners, Free Kicks are all Aerial Passing … Set Plays
  seems to be a boost to the shooting accuracy for direct free kicks. If you
  are hitting it from the half way line into the box you need Aerial Passing"
  [post 35484973, 2011-11-11, mosmosmos]. "AP a pass in the air, set pieces
  acts as a boost for penalties and shooting free kicks … pick a player with
  high AP, ideally high shooting and then set pieces" [post 34678352,
  2011-07-31, jamie1976]. jamie1976 also tested that higher AP makes long free
  kicks reach the forward [post 34688052]. **consensus (pre-S40, restated
  post-S40)**
- A good set-plays taker is "usually more accurate on passes" but misses with
  low shooting [post 35482620, 2011-11-10, mikexvi]. **opinion**
- Set plays "nerfed since the last sim" [post 35484844, 2011-11-11,
  dagriggstar]. "Set plays … a tremendous waste of time" [post 33883982,
  alx460]. Set plays are trained only after everything else is maxed
  [post 33895270; 36082476]. **consensus: low weight**
- Pre-S40 set pieces could be dangerous: "1st goal - set play, basically
  undefendable" [post 34062624, dagriggstar]. In H2H, "any long ball hit to
  the front line with a set piece always finds the target"
  [post 34493129, mosmosmos]. **opinion**
- Practical point: the set-piece bonus belongs to one player per team (the
  taker), so it should be a per-role bonus, not a per-position weight.

### 7. Engine changes around the S40 sim (what moved)

- Old sim: "the most important skills was speed and stamina". New sim:
  "really important skills are: passing, heading, aerial passing"
  [post 35205487, 2011-10-06, shadowwolf666]. "Speed and stamina seem less
  important than before … heading, play intelligence and aerial passing seem
  much more important" [post 35405495, 2011-10-31, innuendo]. "Speed and
  Stamina importance has been removed" [post 35226321, neilillo]. "difference
  in speed and stamina unnoticeable" [post 35307604, nahue85]. A team "built
  around speed and stamina … its all gone now" [post 35571131, bema].
  **consensus (post-S40)**. Staff have not confirmed this.
- Staff: "one of the stated aims of the balancing has been to adjust the
  influence of each stat … In some versions, the balance was wrong"
  [post 35248205, 2011-10-12]. "some stats were boosted too much, whilst
  others not enough" [post 35207160, 2011-10-07]. A keeper with 0 keeping gets
  "4 free balls" [post 35248205]. Weaker players get a small boost in a strong
  team, and players "play best in the position(s) that match their skills"
  [post 35567056, 2011-11-23]. **official**
- Pre-S40, speed and stamina came first: managers cut youths below 6–7
  speed/stamina [topic 9423402, e.g. post 34789814, 34790059, 34792387].
  **consensus (pre-S40)**
- Speed and stamina by position: "right now speed and stamina aren't rated as
  highly for mids as they are for defs and strikers"
  [post 35184206, 2011-10-04, dagriggstar]. **opinion (test sim)**

### Table: tactic option × position → skill weight shifts

Read "+" as the weight goes up versus a neutral baseline and "−" as it goes
down. This is a direction only; no source gives numbers. Confidence:
O = official, C = consensus, P = opinion, I = inference.

| Tactic option | GK | Def (central) | Def (wide/fullback) | Mid (central) | Wing | Striker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Short passing** | AP − (P) | Passing +, PI + (P) | Passing + (P) | Passing ++, PI +, BC + (P/O) | Passing + over AP (P, pre-S40) | BC +, Passing + (P) |
| **Long balls** | AP + (C/P) | AP + but below Tack/Spd/Sta (C, pre-S40) | AP + (I) | Passing −, overall role − (P) | AP + (I) | Speed + (P, pre), BC + to receive (P), Heading + (P), Shooting + for long shots (P) |
| **Wings** | — | Heading + to clear crosses (O: planned patch; currently low) | Speed + vs running wingers (P) | — (wingers in midfield count as Wing) | AP ++, Passing +, Speed +, Stamina +, BC + (O); must be placed wide (O) | Heading ++ (O), contested in practice (C) |
| **Attacking mentality** | — | Speed/Tackling + (I) | Speed + (I) | — | — | — |
| **Defensive mentality** | — | Tackling/PI + (I) | — | — | — | — |
| **Committed pressing** | Stamina + (O for GK stamina) | Stamina +, PI +, Exp + to avoid cards (O) | Stamina + (O/manual) | Stamina +, PI + (O/manual) | Stamina + (manual) | Stamina + (manual) |
| **Passive pressing** | — | Stamina − (manual/P) | Stamina − | Stamina − | Stamina − | Stamina − |
| **3-back / 2-CB formations** | — | central D load + (more actions); PI/Exp + (P) | wide D often idle → position weight − (C, test sim) | holding mid + (P) | — | — |
| **Set-piece taker role** (one player) | AP for goal kicks (P) | AP + if taker (C) | — | AP +, Shooting + (FK) (C) | — | Shooting + Set Plays for direct FK/penalty (C) |

Overall shift from pre-S40 to post-S40 (not tactic specific): Speed and
Stamina went down; AP, Heading, PI and Passing went up (C, unconfirmed by
staff).

## Open questions / contradictions

- **Size of the effects.** No source gives numbers. Post-S40, many posters
  felt 3-ball vs 10-ball differences were too small in every skill, while
  staff said the influence of each stat was still being tuned. Anything after
  2012-02 (later sims) is outside this DB.
- **Long ball vs AP.** The style uses aerial passes, but whether low-AP teams
  are punished is contested: konstas saw 90% completion at 2.5 AP, darkline
  and mosmosmos say AP affects how cleanly the ball is received.
- **Heading:** officially more important, but it does not show in posters'
  tests. Defender heading is officially broken or limited (patch planned).
- **Mentality:** there is no evidence about skill weights at all. The table
  entries are inference.
- **Formations:** "3 defenders need more speed" is not supported. The evidence
  says wide defenders are idle and central defenders do the work, with
  PI/experience for cards. Whether fullbacks were "fixed" in V1.63+ is mixed:
  mosmosmos said yes, while later posts (jauho 10-21, adv41449 10-17) still
  saw idle wide players.
- **Set Plays skill:** the manual says it covers corners, free kicks and
  penalties. The forum consensus is that it covers only direct FK shots and
  penalties, with AP for crossed set pieces. Unresolved.
- **Current game:** the manual still lists only Short/Long/Wings,
  Defensive/Balanced/Attacking and Passive/Normal/Committed, so the option set
  is stable. Weights may have changed in later sims.
