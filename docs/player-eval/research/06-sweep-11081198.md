# Sweep 11081198 … 12109520 (topics 2014-02 … 2017-03)

## Summary
- Current engine lineage: the 2.x sim (live since 2011) got a public beta 2015-12…2016-03 and **V2.40 went live 2016-04-13**; posters call it "an updated version of the live SIM (2.xx), not a new SIM (3.xx)". Changes: strikers in the box prefer shooting, fewer back passes, defenders man-mark/intercept wingers, pressing levels = marking levels, air passes less accurate. [posts 43139745, 43117322, 43118466; Crew posts in topic 11771315]
- **Pressing is now a marking setting** (Crew, V2.29): Passive = nobody man-marks; Normal = defenders man-mark; Committed = defenders + midfielders man-mark. Speed decides who reaches a marking target. → Committed raises midfielder Speed/Tackling relevance, not only Stamina.
- **Wings stayed the dominant tactic 2014–2017**, before and after V2.40 (weaker after). The skills it needs are AP for everyone who passes, plus one header striker and speed. Posters say Stamina, Shooting and Passing were "superseded by Speed, Heading and AP".
- Aerial pass mechanics: the passer's AP decides success; on failure the ball goes to an opponent (Crew V2.32 note). Heading is for directing the ball, so **defender Heading ≈ useless and AP > HEA for defenders**; striker Heading is key under Wings.
- Long balls: poor in 2016–17 ("not up to par", "moderate results"), so its modifiers matter little in practice.
- GK: Keeping dominant. Then Stamina (to ~5–8), PI and AP. BC and Speed are contested, Passing is useless (GK passes are aerial). Tosspot's data (as reported second-hand): only Keeping, Stamina (≤5) and PI correlate with saves.
- Defender: Speed, Stamina, Tackling are the core; the 4th skill is BC, then PI/AP/Passing. Same skills for CB and FB per posters.
- Striker: Shooting, Speed, Stamina, BC, PI are the classic five; Heading is a must under Wings. There are hints that 9–10 SHO strikers dribble to the keeper and waste chances (opinion).
- Stamina: about 10 minutes per ball at Normal pressing (7 lasts 90'); Committed needs about 8–9. Tired players get slower and less accurate.
- Experience: one poster models it as about +0.1 ball per exp ball on all skills (≤ +1 ball). Starters want 7–8+ exp. Form is "match fitness" from match counts.
- Set plays: still unknown. Posters guess shooting from set pieces and corners. No Crew answer.
- No red-card boost (Crew). A tactic-check penalty applies when a team has <11 players at the start or the wrong Def/Mid/Att count after a formation change.

## Findings

### Sim beta 2015-12…2016-03 (patrick7777, topic 11771315)
- Pressing redefined in V2.29 (2015-12-16): Passive = intercept+tackle only; Normal = defenders man-mark, mids intercept/tackle; Committed = defenders AND mids man-mark. Faster players reach marking targets sooner; slower cannot catch faster. [post 42844628, 2015-12-16, patrick7777] **official**
- V2.30–2.32: air passes made less accurate; failed air passes go to an opponent. 'We do not intend to make Wings unusable'. [posts 42847669, 42849200, 42859099, patrick7777] **official**
- 'Training certain Skills will reduce the number of times your Players are stunned' (after failed tackle/fall). [post 42916775, 2016-01-12, patrick7777] **official**
- No explicit red-card boost/penalty; tactic check penalty if <11 players at start or too many/few Att/Mid/Def after formation change. [post 42916775; post 43089854, 2016-03-24, dowopado] **official**
- Topic 11771315 was read in full: all 8 patrick7777 posts (2015-12-05 … 2016-01-15) plus the 62 user replies. More detail from the Crew posts:
  - V2.25–2.27 (2015-12): "Defenders will try to close down the opposition earlier" and are pulled out of position less. "Defenders will not mark other defenders." Defenders who fail a tackle and trip or fall now "recover twice as quickly". A wing-back marking the winger can win the ball high and start attacks ("think Roberto Carlos"). [post 42821053, 2015-12-05, patrick7777] **official**
  - Crew: "If we make defending 'perfect' there would be no goals"; "Players are not 100% perfect". [post 42847669] **official**
  - Strikers passing instead of shooting when 1-on-1 was to be adjusted "once we have settled wings and tackling" (fixed in V2.40: strikers in the box prefer shooting). [posts 42821053, 43118466] **official**
  - Tactic-check penalty: it is cumulative in the live sim and applies only while the failing formation is active (V2.34). It checks <11 players and too many or too few Def/Mid/Att. "Teams who receive a Red Card no longer receive a penalty for having less than 11 Players." [post 42925145, 2016-01-15, patrick7777] **official**. The numeric limits are not given.
  - Red card: the attacker with the lowest shirt number drops back to cover. Losses blamed on red cards come mostly from "lower skill Players moved back to cover". [post 42916775] **official**
- User/GA reports in the same topic (beta, not live):
  - In the beta with committed pressing, strikers made the most tackle attempts: "3 strikers--19 tackling attempts, 4 backline defenders--9" (strikers had 2 TAC balls) [post 42927084, 2016-01-15, dowopado]. → Under committed pressing, forwards' TAC gets used. **opinion (single match)**
  - High defensive lines up to halfway "means there is a definite need for defenders with high speed and stamina", plus PI/exp to drop back [post 42988078, 2016-02-09, dowopado] **opinion**
  - V2.37/2.38 cut AP effectiveness "drastically", which "rendered the wing tactic almost useless". V2.40 partly restored it; Crew "has no intentions of making wing tactics un-useable" [post 43029038, 2016-02-26, dowopado] **opinion relaying Crew**
  - Aerial passes have the same success chance at 15 m or 70 m, and keepers' aerial passes follow the same rules as outfield players' [post 42928424, 2016-01-16, scruttino] **opinion**. → GK AP = outfield AP mechanically.
  - Beta results were "fairly consistent" across short and wings: wing vs short 13-0-5, short vs wings 4-1-1 [post 42942804, 2016-01-21, dowopado] **opinion (data point)**

### Wings era 2013–2016 and the 2016-04 sim
- New sim went live 2016-04-13 ("Matches booked after 1315 CET will use the new SIM"), after the beta above. [post 43139745, 2016-04-13, dowopado quoting news] **official (news)**
- Pre-2016 (live sim ~2012–2016) Wings was the dominant tactic; teams with "very few, but specific skills" beat stronger short-passing teams. [post 41945717, 2015-01-24, tosspot; post 41952572, 2015-01-26, tonnerwhu: "they don't have a handful players +7 AP"] **consensus**
- "Skills like Stamina, Shooting and Passing have been superseded by Speed, Heading and AP" (for Wings). [post 41660308, 2014-11-03, aj69] **consensus** (mosmosmos, navai, tonnerwhu agree)
- navai's sims vs wing teams: "strikers don't need shooting anymore, they need heading, midfielders don't need short passing, they need aerial passing, defenders … need heading and tackling"; tonnerwhu disputes defender heading. [post 41966195 / 41966352, 2015-01-30] **contested** (defender HEA)
- Real numbers, EPL-winning Wings team S46 (10 outfield avg): SPD 8.8, STA 7.2, PI 6.2, PAS 7.1, BC 7.8, TAC 8.1, AP 7.7; lone striker SHO 10, HEA 8. [post 41950666, 2015-01-26, phaedrus] **opinion (data point)**
- Example wing team that killed top teams: 6-7 Spd/Sta, 0-5 PI/Pas, 6-7 BC/Tac, but all 7-9 AP + one fast winger + header striker. [post 41660245, 2014-11-03, mosmosmos] **opinion**
- 2015 World Cup semi-finalists: 5-3-2 "spamming a lone striker with crosses", players "9/10 speed, stamina, PI, BC, AP and tackling". [post 42734794, 2015-10-27, scruttino] **opinion**
- Still in 2017 (new sim): "the majority of successful teams these days play wings. you do need effective aerial passers throughout your team … and a great header of the ball in front" [post 43670747, 2017-01-17, smiller29]; a wing team with "poor speed, stamina and ball control but exceptional aerial passing, tackling and a front man [header]" beat a better short-passing team; "not quite as bad as it once was" [post 43670843, 2017-01-17, andyferg88]. **consensus**
- Mechanic (poster): "With aerial passes who will receive the ball/win the header is decided when the ball leaves the foot … successful pass picks your player, unsuccessful passes seek out an opposition player. It rarely goes into space." Heading: "Players still don't compete with each other to win headers, the skill is purely about directing it." [posts 43462619, 43461352, 2016-09-25, jrook] **opinion**, consistent with Crew beta V2.32 note ("When a Player misses … with an air pass, the ball will instead target an opposition Player") [post 42859099]. → AP of the *passer* decides success; defender HEA does not win duels.
- Heading 2016: important for strikers under Wings; for defenders "questionable"; other positions irrelevant. [post 43461241, 2016-09-24, dowopado] **consensus** (troyh65, jrook, smiller29: train AP over HEA for defenders)
- Headers were nerfed in some update before 2015-04 ("headers were too overpowered"). [post 42175745, 2015-04-09, adv41449] **opinion**
- Sim: pass length doesn't change success chance ("same chances … over 15 metres or 70 metres"). [post 43172505, 2016-04-29, scruttino] **opinion**

### Positions / skills (2014–2017 posters)
- GK: Tosspot's external tool, "100,000's of matches": saves correlate strongly with Keeping; Stamina positive but negligible above 5; PI positive but very small; no other skill (BC) correlated. [post 43163707, 2016-04-25, theme_dreamer reporting Tosspot] **opinion (secondhand data)**; contested by matttombstone/smiller29 (BC stops rebounds) [posts 43162501, 43166350]. GK AP "crucial and much underrated, big difference between 3 AP and 8 AP" [post 43172736, held85]; "all keeping passes are aerial so passing is not needed" [post 43172505, scruttino]; passing useless [43162501]. Consensus summary: "Keeping, Stamina, PI and AP are quite important. Most seem to think BC and speed also help" [post 43174528, 2016-04-30, matttombstone]. **consensus**

- Striker: classic formula "shoot / speed / stamina / ball control / intelligence … lately the heading come into the picture" [post 41858229, 2015-01-02, aty]; top scorers in ARG/BRA with only 7 BC / 6 PI. Main striker under wings: train heading; supporting striker: tackling/AP [posts 41803773, 41808859]. **consensus**
- PI: debated; many see PI as important for central mid, GK, defenders, less for wingers/strikers [post 42938441, 2016-01-19]; "PI, as speed and resistence [stamina], is a boost skill" [post 42947275, civilu]. **contested**

- Experience: posters want 7–8+ exp for regular starters [post 43299856, 2016-07-03, theme_dreamer] **opinion**
- Form ≈ match fitness (not results); 2 league + 4 friendlies/week keeps 9 form; cups only positive [post 43362406, 2016-07-31, matttombstone; post 44291768, evosa table]. **consensus**
- Pressing labels renamed: old "Style" aggressive/normal/… → Pressing committed/normal/passive (new tactic editor, 2016) [post 43184420]. Committed = "better performances … saps stamina much faster" **opinion**

## Changes to RESULTS.md
1. **Key facts table: add the V2.40 sim (live 2016-04-13)** as the current engine and note that 2014–17 posts describe it. Release notes: strikers shoot more in the box, defenders man-mark wingers, air passes are less accurate, failed air passes go to opponents. [posts 43139745, 43118466; topic 11771315 patrick7777] **official**
2. **Pressing modifiers: re-base them on marking** (Crew V2.29 definitions, post 42844628):
   - Committed: CM/W `SPD×1.15, TAC×1.15` on top of STA×1.25 (midfielders now man-mark). CD/FB keep their STA factor, plus `SPD×1.1`.
   - Normal: CD/FB `SPD×1.1` relative to Passive (defenders man-mark, and faster players reach targets first).
   - Passive: drop SPD for CD/FB a little (`SPD×0.9`). Intercepting still counts, so keep PI.
   - Committed: also a small ST `TAC×1.1`; in the beta, strikers made the most tackle attempts under committed pressing [post 42927084] **opinion**.
   - Raise the committed STA factor: posters need about 8–9 STA to last 90' on committed versus 7 on normal [posts 43184420, 42693076, 42693209] **consensus**.
3. **Wings modifiers stronger, and AP for passers**: under Wings, AP is needed "throughout your team" [43670747, 43670843, 41952572]. Proposal: Wings CD/FB `AP×1.4`, CM `AP×1.4, PAS×0.8`, W `AP×1.5` (from 1.4), ST `HEA×1.8` (from 1.6) and `SHO×0.9`. **consensus**, but nerfed by V2.40 ("not quite as bad as it once was"), so stop at these values.
4. **Defender Heading down**: CD HEA .05 → .02, and drop `HEA×1.3` for CD under Wings (or cut it to ×1.1). Heading doesn't win duels; AP > HEA for defenders [43462619, 43461241, 43462691; contested by navai 41966195]. Move the freed weight to AP (CD AP .05 → .08).
5. **GK**: PAS stays 0. Raise AP to .07–.08 (GK passes are all aerial: posts 43172505, 43172736). Lower SPD to .05 and BC to .08 (contested, and Tosspot's data shows no correlation). Add a STA cap: `shape` for GK STA saturates above ~6 (Tosspot: negligible effect above 5) [post 43163707]. **opinion/consensus mix**
6. **Long balls**: the modifiers can stay, but flag the style as weak in 2016–17 [posts 43741438, 43749353, 42885532, 43318296]. **consensus**
7. **Experience**: E = 0.05 may be low. One poster's model is +0.1 ball per exp ball ≈ +1 ball at 10 exp ≈ +10–12% on 8–9 ball skills, mostly via PI/decisions [post 43150806, matttombstone]. Proposal: E = 0.08, still a placeholder. Posters' rule: starters need 7+ exp [43299856, 43295102]. **opinion**
8. **ST**: keep HEA small in the base weights (short passing) but large under Wings. Add a note that SHO above ~8 may have diminishing returns (claim: 9–10 SHO strikers run to the keeper) [post 43546922] **opinion**. This supports the existing `shape()` concavity and is not a reason to change the weights.
9. **Contradictions section updates**: "Heading: Crew says far more important…" is now resolved toward *striker yes, defender no* (2016 posters). AP under Long balls stays moot because Long balls is rarely used. Defender Speed: Crew's marking mechanic makes Speed matter for defenders under Normal/Committed pressing.
