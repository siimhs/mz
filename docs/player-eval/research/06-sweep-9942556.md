# Sweep 9942556 … 11081198 (topics started 2012-04 … 2014-02)

Scope: 2,732 topic titles (forums 10 + 12) scanned, 272 candidates, 62 read plus
other topics found by full-text search. None were in `covered-topics.json`.
Sim context: these posts come after the S40 sim (2011-10). Posters report 3 sim
changes in seasons 39-41 [post 39304918, 2013-05-13, pho_oenix]. Crew name a
"V2.18" with beta matches and announce a beta sim update in 2013-12
[post 40380706, 2013-12-09, patrick7777]. No Crew post in this range gives skill
weights. Crew posts are mostly admin, apart from the 2013-12 sim feedback call.

## Summary
1. **Overall quality beats single skills.** Posters' tests find 10 vs 8 Shooting,
   10 vs 8 Tackling and swapped DEF/ST lines make little difference. "Total balls incl.
   experience" decides matches (consensus, tests are small).
2. **Shooting saturates against good keepers.** A 10-SHO striker scored 2.5× an
   8-SHO striker against a 0-KEE keeper, and no more against a 10-KEE keeper (opinion, one tester's data).
3. **Stamina acts as a match-long multiplier on all skills.** Skills fade as the
   player tires. 7 is the common floor and more still helps (consensus).
4. **Core outfield four = Speed, Stamina, PI, BC.** World-class minimums are about
   Spd 9 / Sta 8 / PI 7 / BC 8. Then 8-9+ in the position skill (TAC or SHO) (consensus).
5. **Speed is still a top skill** in 2012-14: minimum 8 for D1, and 7 is "not good enough"
   (consensus; one veteran says it matters less than in ~2005).
6. **Defenders**: TAC ≥ 9 required, with Spd ≥ 8. Tackle success tracks PI more than BC.
   BC lets the defender keep the ball after a tackle. Passing matters because the defender passes after every
   tackle (consensus/opinion mix).
7. **Heading**: no evidence it helps defenders ("defensive headers non-existent").
   It helps the central forward under **Wings** only (consensus).
8. **Wings tactic (2013)** became very strong with fast wingers (Spd, Sta, AP, maybe BC)
   and a heading striker. Crew acknowledged it ("wingers cannot be tackled once
   running", "low AP wingers still achieve too many successful crosses") (official).
9. **Long balls**: no good reports in this sim. The ball goes "along the ground" (opinion ×2).
10. **Experience**: posters say this sim "heavily emphasises experience" (opinion ×3,
    against the 2011 Crew statement that its weight was reduced). Contested.
11. **GK**: KEE first, then PI ≈ Sta (poll: PI 12, Sta 10, Spd 3, AP 1). BC and Spd
    come after. Keepers with secondaries save ~83% of shots vs ~70% for keepers without (consensus + data).
    The MZ Assistant tool's GK rating uses KEE .55, Spd/Sta/BC/PI .09, PAS/AP .02, Exp .05.
12. **Set Plays**: no visible effect for takers. AP is used for free kicks and goal kicks (opinion).
13. **No effect**: height/weight, captain, and team-form graph. Only the on-field players' form counts
    (consensus; Patrick via hearsay).

## Findings

### Skill impact tests (2012-09 … 2013-01)
- 10 vs 8 SHO strikers, about 20 matches each in both positions: no difference (12 vs 17
  goals from ~100 shots each) [post 37764161, 2012-09-23, brie] – opinion (test).
- Same tester: against own 10-KEE keeper, 8-SHO striker 3 goals / 45 shots vs 10-SHO 2 / 22.
  Against a 0-KEE stand-in keeper: 8-SHO 4/18, 10-SHO 10/25 ("2.5-fold") [post 37806779,
  2012-09-29, brie] – opinion. Interpretation: shooting matters against weak keepers and is
  capped by strong keeping.
- tosspot (395 matches) does see a SHO-goals correlation [post 37764525, 2012-09-23,
  tosspot]. mosmosmos sees "little difference between my 8, 9 & 10" [post 37772049,
  2012-09-25, mosmosmos] – contested.
- Tackling 10 vs 8 on the same line: 49% vs 48% success [post 37765546, 2012-09-23,
  baltovenaar]. 9-TAC defenders with more PI: 56% vs 52% [post 37765593, 2012-09-23,
  tosspot]. MZA 50 games: 6-BC 50% vs 9-BC 52%; best tacklers are the 8-10 PI defenders
  (59-60%) [posts 39395476, 39395499, 2013-05-30, darkline] – consensus that PI > BC
  for tackle success.
- Strikers placed in defence and defenders in attack (international level) vs the main
  tactic in a tactic test: "repeatedly … an even match … Draw" [post 38444316,
  2013-01-03, topic 10432231] – opinion (single test).
- "Now it's more the total count of balls including experience", while the previous sim
  rewarded single top skills [post 37775565, 2012-09-25, belaware] – opinion, echoed by
  [post 38766447, 2013-02-16, dowopado].
- Marcus (main sim developer) interview, quoted: agent-based AI, "nothing … predestined".
  Out of 100 games the better team "(including player skills, form, tactics, etc.)" should
  win the most [post 37783190, 2012-09-26, darkline quoting the USA Zone] – official
  (old interview).

### Stamina
- "As Stamina reduces during a game, other skills also degrade … It affects all skills".
  With 10 vs 5 Sta, the 10-Sta player is noticeably better after an hour [post 38325581,
  2012-12-19, mosmosmos] – consensus (mentaldog guide says the same:
  a 10-SHO 5-Sta player "finish[es] the match as a 9-7 shooter" [post 38650096,
  2013-01-30, heddo quoting mentaldog]).
- 7 Sta = decent for 90 minutes, and more is still helpful [posts 37772049, 37773034,
  2012-09-25, mosmosmos/tosspot] – consensus.
- Committed pressing: opponents "batter" for 20-30 min, then run out of steam [post
  38461031, 2013-01-07, mosmosmos] – opinion. Supports STA× under committed.
- GK stamina: "very little difference to keepers at the moment" [post 38650692,
  2013-01-30, tosspot]. Patrick reportedly said keepers use stamina and 6 is a good level
  [post 38662893, 2013-02-01, darkline] – hearsay.

### Play Intelligence
- PI boosts the other skills (not Spd/Sta/Exp) and is a must for all positions [post 38903803,
  2013-03-09, civilu]. It improves the timing and choice of pass/shot/tackle, which is the same as a boost on all
  skills [post 38915514, 2013-03-11, tosspot] – consensus.
- More specific view: PI changes which way the player turns after receiving and helps him evade his man
  [posts 38324298, 38844569, 2012-12/2013-02, mosmosmos] – opinion.
- Some managers prefer ~6 PI strikers because high-PI strikers pass instead of shooting [post 38903556,
  2013-03-09, qpegasus; post 37801087, 2012-09-28, mawi] – opinion.

### Positions
- **Outfield minimums**: 8 Spd / 7 Sta / 7 BC, then 8 SHO or 8 TAC by position. Mids are
  balanced, and failed DEF/ST who max at 6-7 TAC/SHO become mids [post 40173727, 2013-10-25,
  welshkamikaze]. World class: Spd 9, Sta 8, PI 7, BC 8 minimum [post 38942857,
  2013-03-16, tosspot], and core sum ≥ 33 is "fairly accurate" [post 38947122] – consensus.
- **Speed**: "one of the most important skills in the game, along with stamina and
  passing" [post 36706398, 2012-04-24, trinity]. "Minimum 8 all round" [post 38144713,
  2012-11-18, mosmosmos]. 7 Spd is not top-division level [post 40559531, 2014-01-14,
  damo11] – consensus. Dissent: speed is less crucial than ~2005 [post 39891116, 2013-08-29,
  matttombstone].
- **CD**: 9 TAC minimum. An 8 Spd / 8 TAC defender "won't hold up", so convert him to a mid
  [posts 40019884, 40019997, 40021701, 2013-09-26] – consensus. mentaldog DEF tiers:
  must = Spd, Sta, TAC; needed = AP, PI, PAS; bonus = BC [post 40381756, 2013-12-10,
  scruttino]. BC 7 minimum, 8-9 preferred, but a BC ball can be swapped for +1 Spd or PI [post
  40383159, 2013-12-10, mosmosmos]. BC = cleaner tackles, PI = better-timed tackles,
  PAS because "defender has to pass the ball every time he makes a tackle" [post 38328851,
  2012-12-19, mosmosmos] – opinion.
- **FB**: wide defenders need speed and tackling to catch wingers [post 39464085, 2013-06-13,
  solarmkd]. Fullbacks don't overlap ("defenders moving off the ball just doesn't happen")
  [post 37057355, 2012-06-17, mosmosmos]. A 4-man back line works against wingers [post 39391296,
  2013-05-31, baltovenaar] – consensus.
- **CM/DM**: TAC, PAS, BC, PI; SHO for AM; AP for all mids under Wings; Spd 6-7, Sta 7-8
  [post 37390457, 2012-07-30, brobaek]. Spd 8, and PAS and AP are "equally important" [post 37400226,
  2012-07-31, dowopado]. DMs are picked by PAS/AP [post 39459632, 2013-06-12, dowopado].
- **W**: Spd + Sta + AP make wingers "pretty much unstoppable" [post 39388375, 2013-05-29,
  tonnerwhu]. BC: an 8 Spd / 6 BC / 9 AP winger "was getting tackled all the time", while a 10 Spd / 9 BC
  player worked [post 39390412, 2013-05-29, darkline]. mosmosmos: BC is not a winger skill
  [post 39394172] – contested. Crew's sim-bug template lists the winger's **AP, PI, BC, SP**
  [post 40366740, 2013-12-06, patrick7777 via dowopado] – official (the attributes Crew consider).
- **ST**: Shooting contested (see tests). BC raises striker output [post 37764208,
  2012-09-23, mosmosmos]. Heading is only useful under Wings (next section).
- **GK**: poll of the most important secondary: PI 12, Sta 10, Spd 3, AP 1 [post 40022150,
  2013-09-27, tammel]. RO NT manager: KEE > Exp > PI > Sta 7-8 > Spd; BC/AP
  for distribution; never saw SP help [post 38790911, 2013-02-19, civilu]. Data: 39 10-KEE keepers,
  12,000 matches, average save 82.9% (max 88.4%). 10-KEE with barely any secondaries ~70%. "secondaries
  certainly help" [posts 38781374, 38789332, 2013-02-18/19, tosspot] – consensus + data.
  MZ Assistant GK rating per ball: KEE .55, Spd/Sta/BC/PI .09, PAS & AP .02, Exp .05
  [post 38792117, 2013-02-19, rookerman] – third-party tool.

  2013-10-07, tonnerwhu]. CB with 8 HEA "exactly the same" [post 39423564, 2013-06-05,

- Heading for forwards: "seems to help for Wing Play tactics", not Short [post 40077853,
  2013-10-07, mosmosmos]. A Top League winner used a 7-8+ HEA striker with a 7-8+ AP winger [post
  40078089, 2013-10-07, manolo_jimenez] – consensus. Dissent: a 5-HEA striker was the 2nd top scorer under
  wings [post 39426844, 2013-06-06, rivercampion].
- Height/weight: random and cosmetic [posts 38101335, 39357381, 38768602] – consensus.
- Set Plays: 1-2 SP takers are as good as 6-7 [post 39878043, 2013-08-26, dowopado]. AP, not SP,
  for free kicks and goal kicks [posts 39269930, 39897012, 2013, mosmosmos] – opinion. Subs and keepers
  don't take set pieces [post 40390220, 2013-12-12, tosspot].
- Captain: "a title in name only" [post 39852585, 2013-08-21, scruttino] – consensus.

### Tactics
- Short passing is the norm and best for tight formations. Wings suits wing formations. "haven't seen any
  good reports on long passing in this sim" [post 40279189, 2013-11-16, dowopado]. Long balls
  go "along the ground", not lobbed [post 37410502, 2012-08-02, jaysargey] – opinion ×2.
- Meta: 2012-10 wings "doesn't work" and 3-2 back lines dominate [post 37869994, 2012-10-08,
  aspher]. By 2013-06 wings with top wingers beats 3-2 back lines. The counter is a 4-man line or stacking
  defenders "who can kick the ball long" [posts 39422246, 39424598, 2013-06-05]. "no
  difference between a great defender and a poor one when it comes to defending crossings"
  [post 39425053, 2013-06-05, darkline]. In the previous sim AP passes were ~95-100% accurate regardless of
  skill [post 39425047, 2013-06-05, mosmosmos].
- Crew 2013-12 reference-match call: 1v1 chances saved too often; "difficult to stop wing
  play, as wingers 'cannot' be tackled once they are running"; "Low AP wingers still achieve
  too many successful crosses" [post 40366740, 2013-12-06] – official (known imbalances
  of the then-current sim).
- 2013-12: no engine change. ITC rounded players out, so short-passing teams' AP went from ~0 to 6-9 and
  their aerial passes now find targets [post 40471527, 2013-12-27, mosmosmos] – opinion.
- Tackling aggression can't be changed in-match; style and mentality can [post 39024025,
  2013-03-30, tosspot]. Mentality: trial and error only [post 40490756, 2013-12-31].

  Experience gives "a little boost in all your skills" and fewer cards [posts 38765693, 38766447, 2013-02-16]

  out what the current form calculation is" [post 38048229, 2012-11-02, patrick7777].

## Changes to RESULTS.md
1. **Stamina as a fatigue multiplier** (new term). Keep the STA weight, but also scale the other skills
   by a stamina-dependent match-average factor. Example: `fat = 1 - 0.03*max(0, 7 - STA)`
   for outfield players, ~0 for GK. Cite 38325581, 38650096, 37772049/37773034, 38650692.
2. **Add a generalist / "total quality" component**: `score = 0.75*positionScore +
   0.25*mean(SPD, STA, PI, BC)` (GK excluded, or uses its own secondaries). Cite 37765546,
   38444316, 37775565, 38942857, 40173727. Tag opinion; calibrate.
3. **ST Shooting .22 → .18**. Give +.02 to STA and +.02 to BC, and note an optional
   opponent-keeper interaction: SHO matters more against weak keepers. Cite 37764161,
   37806779, 37772049 (contested with 37764525).
4. **CD Heading .05 → .02** (+.02 PI, +.01 PAS), and **drop Wings HEA×1.3 for CD/FB**.
   Cite 37726273, 38098309, 40077896, 39425053. Keep ST HEA×1.6 under Wings (40077853,
   40078089).
5. **Long-ball AP multipliers down**: GK 1.5→1.25, CD 1.6→1.3, FB 1.4→1.2, CM 1.3→1.15.
   Cite 40279189, 37410502 (plus the round-1 AP 2.5 test).
6. **GK**: PI .10→.12, STA .10→.11, BC .10→.09, AP .05→.04, SP .02→.00. Optionally add
   Exp to the GK cond term (MZA .05/ball). Cite 40022150, 38790911, 38792117, 39269930.
7. **CM Speed .12→.14** (take −.02 from SHO). **FB/CD Speed unchanged** (consensus that it is high).
   Cite 36706398, 38144713, 40559531, 37400226.
8. **Experience E 0.05 → 0.08**, marked contested (2011 Crew "reduced" vs 2012-13 posters
   "heavy emphasis"). Cite 37608002, 38766447, 37775565, 38790911.
9. **Wings modifier for W**: add STA×1.1. Keep AP×1.4 and SPD×1.1, and add BC×1.05 only as a
   contested option. Cite 39388375, 39390412, 39394172, 40366740. Note that Crew in 2013-12
   flagged AP as *under*-effective for crosses, so don't push AP higher.
10. **Set-piece score**: `0.35*SP + 0.35*AP + 0.3*SHO` (was 0.5/0.2/0.3) [39878043,
    39897012, 38790911].
11. **Key facts table**: add "captain has no effect" (39852585), "team-form graph has no
    effect; only on-field player form counts" (37035763), and "tackle success tracks PI, not BC"
    (39395476, 37765593). Re-confirm height/weight unused (38101335).

- Experience: 2011 Crew "reduced" vs 2012-13 posters "heavy emphasis".
- The sim was tuned several times in S39-41 and a beta update was announced 2013-12, so the
  wings meta of 2013 may not hold later.
