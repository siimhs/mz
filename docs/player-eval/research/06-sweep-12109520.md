# Sweep 12109520 … 13146945 (topics 2017-03 … 2026-11, current engine)

2,732 titles scanned (forums 10 + 12). None were in `covered-topics.json`. Crew
posters in range: powdersnow (354 posts), thunderexpress (115), mikelony (34).
None of their posts give skill weights or engine internals. The Crew posts here
are about features, events and the transfer market.

Engine status: posters agree the match sim has had **no real update since the
2011 S40 release**. "nothing has changed for 13 years" [post 46176796,
2023-12-19, hooterman]; "we had this SIM for 15 years" [post 46435375,
2025-08-12, darkline]. **consensus**. So the post-2012 evidence below describes
the same engine as round 1's post-S40 posts, with 10+ more years of player
experience behind it. There are hints of later parameter tweaks: penalties went
from "almost every one a goal" to ">90% saved" [post 46439771, 2025-08-26,
civilu], and wings became less dominant by 2026 (see below).

## Summary

- **Experience is the strongest factor in the current engine** (consensus
  2017–2025). Seniors need 9–10 Exp to compete. This contradicts RESULTS'
  "Exp ≤ +5%" placeholder.
- **PI is a top-3 skill for GK, DEF, DM and MID.** Top teams field almost no
  one under 8–9 PI. Posters call it an "amplifier" (consensus). For wingers and
  strikers it matters less, and high PI may even make them pass back instead of
  crossing (opinion ×3).
- 2018 community poll (aurelrabei, 6 threads, 7–27 ballots each), mean rank
  (1 = most important):
  - GK: KEE 1.0 · STA 2.9 · PI 3.0 · EXP 4.6 · AP 5.4 · BC 5.9 · SPD 6.0 · PAS 8.0 · SP 8.3
  - DEF: TAC 1.7 · SPD 3.2 · STA 3.2 · PI 3.9 · EXP 4.9 · BC 5.5 · AP 6.5 · PAS 7.8 · HEA 8.4
  - DM: TAC 2.9 · PI 3.8 · SPD 4.3 · STA 4.3 · EXP 4.8 · AP 5.3 · BC 6.1 · PAS 6.3 · HEA 8.6
  - MID: PI 2.6 · BC 3.0 · STA 3.7 · EXP 4.2 · PAS 5.8 · SPD 5.9 · TAC 5.9 · AP 6.3 · SHO 8.1
  - WING: SPD 2.0 · AP 2.6 · STA 4.0 · BC 4.2 · PI 4.2 · EXP 5.5 · PAS 6.9 · TAC 7.5 · SHO 8.0
  - ST: SHO 2.9 · PI 3.7 · STA 4.4 · BC 4.6 · SPD 4.6 · HEA 4.9 · EXP 5.0 · PAS 7.1 · TAC 8.5 · AP 8.9
- **Wings** was the dominant style 2017–2024. It needs only one good AP winger
  plus one or two heading strikers, while Short needs 10 good field players. It
  converts about 1 in 2–3 shots against 10+ for Short (consensus). By 2026 Wings
  and Short were "near balance" and top teams switch between them mid-match
  (opinion ×2).
- **Long balls "never really worked"**. One poster says it plays like "wide
  short passing" (consensus). Long-ball modifiers should be small.
- **Defender Heading is nearly useless**: defenders rarely contest crosses.
  Against Wings, Tackling + PI (and stopping the winger) matter more
  (contested, leaning low).
- **Speed is overrated** (opinion ×4). The new 2024 stats show average speed
  differs by only ~3 km/h between 3 and 8–9 balls, and not at all between 6 and
  10. Sprints may still matter.
- **Shooting has a weak individual effect**. Season data show low-shooting mids
  converting as well as 9–10-shooting strikers (opinion with data ×3).
- **Stamina thresholds**: 8+ for DEF/MID and 7+ for FW at the top level. A
  player with 6–7 STA lasts ≤ 30 min at a high level. Committed pressing needs
  8–9 (consensus).
- **Form**: don't field anyone under form 9. Form 7–8 means ~20% worse
  performance (opinion ×2).
- **"Total balls" belief**: many top managers think the sim uses team or
  regional total skill balls, so "useless" balls also help. National teams are
  full of 80+-ball players. This belief has never been tested (consensus belief).
- GK: one Keeping ball is worth more than one ball of any outfield main skill
  (opinion). Exp counts more than secondaries. There is a "random keeper
  behaviour" factor (opinion).
- Since 2024-11-25 the match page has **detailed stats**: passes, failed passes,
  tackles, assists, saves, distance and average speed. They can serve as
  calibration targets (official, Crew).

## Findings

### Engine and tactics
- No sim update since ~2011: [post 46176796, 2023-12-19, hooterman],
  [post 46179948, 2023-12-24, ruisimoes] (staff said changing the sim lost users),
  [post 46435375, 2025-08-12, darkline]. **consensus**
- Wings needs fewer good players. "if you play short passing you need 10 good
  field players, if you play wings you can be competitive just by having a good
  winger, one or two good strikers with heading" [post 46224015, 2024-03-16,
  darkline]. Same claim: [post 45839036, 2022-04-21, brock123] (9/10 AP winger
  + 9/10 heading CF), [post 46284116, 2024-07-24, k33n3y]. **consensus**
- Wings conversion: "wings scores 1 in 3 or 1 in 2 shots and SP you need about
  10 shots+" [post 46237338, 2024-04-11, hooper14]. "Wings score more goals per
  attempt, but SP create more attempts" [post 45799187, 2022-02-21, blavitt_jens].
  The Wings chain is winger pass 66% → dribble 66% → cross 50% → shot 15%, and
  fewer passes mean fewer interceptions [post 45845776, 2022-05-04, dagriggstar
  (RA)]. **consensus**
- 2026 balance: "Today, however, there is a near-balance between wing play and
  short passing … Long passing has never really worked very well" [post 46566556,
  2026-09-16, tiger13]. "short passes now reign supreme" in U18 [post 46567105,
  2026-09-17, kochas (RA)]. The opposite view: Wings is "overwhelmingly dominant"
  [post 46564738, 2026-09-11, camouflage38 (LA)]. **contested (recent)**
- Long balls "plays like wide short passing" [post 45798774, 2022-02-20,
  dagriggstar (RA)]. "so poorly implemented … no one even remembers it exists"
  [post 45798913, 2022-02-20, darkline]. **consensus**
- Defensive line against style: a 3-back line is good against Short and bad
  against Wings, a 4-back line is neutral, and a 5-back line is good against
  Wings and bad against Short [post 45799225, 2022-02-21, jere92]. Five
  defenders against Wings: [post 44081565, 2017-09-22, scruttino].
  **consensus**
- Committed pressing means 1v1 marking with immediate engagement
  [post 45510286, 2021-02-12, chucky06]. It needs STA 8 minimum and 9 ideally
  [post 45303193, 2020-07-06, chucky06]. **opinion**
- Defensive mentality: "my defenders don't stray as much and also tackle better"
  [post 44435678, 2018-06-12, killerbeeez (NC)], and defensive gave better
  results [post 44461300, 2018-06-30, alx_is_back (FA)]. Against this: "sim is
  not complex enough" [post 44435694, aldebaran]. **contested**
- A high defensive line works with 9 SPD / 8 STA defenders [post 44038345,
  2017-08-22, civilu]. Offsides are extremely rare [post 44041088, hanzinho].
  **opinion**
- A player never passes back to the teammate he received the ball from
  [post 46437600, 2025-08-19, nukedheart]. **opinion**
- A competition "boost" for weaker teams [post 46264363, 2024-06-14, mihairo].
  This is a team effect and out of scope. **opinion**

### Experience, form, total balls
- "At the moment experience is the strongest skill in the game, if you want to
  compete for senior trophies … all your players need to have 10 in experience"
  [post 45403148, 2020-10-15, scruttino]. "I have players with 6 Pi … no issue
  but I can't use players with anything less than 9 experience … the less
  experience the more random results" [post 45416523, 2020-11-01, darkline].
  "experience is too important" for 23–24-year-olds with 6–7 Exp
  [post 46184247, 2024-01-02, filipek4]. Exp has more effect than PI
  [post 45430421, 2020-11-17, yenscallens123]. Strikers and keepers feel low Exp
  most [post 45416477, 2020-11-01, webregas]. **consensus**
- In the 2018 poll Exp ranks 4th–5th at every position, ahead of AP, BC and
  Passing for most positions (see Summary). **consensus**
- Exp reaches 10 at about age 26–27 with regular play [post 45441802,
  2020-11-30, jere92]. Bench time counts too [post 46351910, 2025-01-02].
- Form: "If you get 7-8 form you will have around 20% worse preformance … compared
  to a 9.75 form player" [post 45818363, 2022-03-20, swaan1]. Field no one
  under form 9 [post 46343277, 2024-12-15, aquarius]. Poor strikers' results
  may come from low form when they play no friendlies [post 46407999,
  2025-05-28, aquarius]. **opinion ×2**
- Total balls: "strong managers believe players are better with more balls, even
  if for skills which don't matter for the specific position" [post 44155393,
  2017-11-08, civilu]. "I go for total amount of balls + 9 experience"
  [post 45983977, 2022-12-15, darkline]. "A large amount of overall balls seems
  to be more important" than PI [post 46237059, 2024-04-10, mwosle (FA)].
  National teams are full of 80+-ball players [post 46351910, 2025-01-02].
  **consensus belief, untested**

### Skills
- PI: "Most important skill for all defenders and midfielders. Not as important
  for wingers and strikers" [post 45769237, 2022-01-10, chucky06]. Top teams use
  no one under 8–9 PI [posts 45246828, 45246926, 2020-05-18]. All players need
  9–10 PI to challenge the best [post 45695583, 2021-09-29, chucky06]. High PI
  means fewer fouls and cards and more interceptions [post 45242468, 2020-05-14,
  evosa]. PI is a "must" for GK and heading strikers [post 45766022, 2022-01-07,
  kevinmicha (LA)]. **consensus**. Against this, one top team has 7-PI players
  [post 46237059, 2024, mwosle (FA)].
- PI may hurt W/ST: high-PI wingers and strikers pass more and too early
  [post 45780114, 2022-01-25, chucky06]. High-PI wingers run back instead of
  crossing [posts 46505612, 46505669, 2026-03-06]. **opinion ×3**
- Speed: "Speed is currently the most overrated skill … 7-8 speed players
  constantly keep up with 9-10 speed players" [post 44530973, 2018-08-22,
  chucky06]. 2024 stats show 3 against 8–9 balls ≈ 3 km/h average
  [post 46335339, 2024-11-25, big_green_shoe], and 6 against 10 shows "no
  difference" [post 46335995, meser]. Average speed hides sprints
  [post 46336023, darkline]. A 7-speed player outpaced a 9-speed one
  [post 46558063, 2026-08-19]. Against this: Speed ranks #1 for wingers and #2
  for defenders in the poll, and 18 of 20 TL national-team seniors have 8+
  Speed and Stamina [post 46501053, 2026-02-19, romanasu]. **contested**
- Shooting: a season of league data shows a low-shooting midfielder had the best
  conversion [topic 12892951, 2022-06/07, dagriggstar (RA)]. Mids with few
  Shooting balls convert like 9–10-shooting strikers [post 45881401, 2022-06-27].
  Strikers with 6–7 Shooting outscore 9–10 ones [post 46403450, 2025-05-14,
  mike2kcool; post 46407735, viedras20]. Still, Shooting ranks #1 for ST in the
  poll. **contested**
- Heading (defenders): "defenders never challenge a striker for the ball in the
  air" [post 44077858, 2017-09-20, scruttino; post 45100870, 2019-12-19]. The
  odds of a CB heading away a cross are "very, very low", and ≤ 1/3 of Wings
  goals are headers [post 44093498, 2017-09-28, dowopado]. "Useless"
  [post 45760352, 2021-12-31], later "might have an effect" [post 45936461,
  2022-09-23]. Wingbacks do intercept crosses with headers [post 45760460,
  jere92]. Full-backs need Speed, AP and Heading [post 46008326, 2023-01-22,
  k33n3y]. Poll: HEA ranks last for DEF (8.4). **contested, leaning low**
- Heading (ST): needed for Wings strikers. Heading potential sets a Wings
  striker's market price [post 45366130, 2020-09-09, scruttino]. **consensus**
- BC: a senior striker needs at least 7 BC [post 45302409, 2020-07-05,
  chucky06]. Short-passing players need more BC than wingers because they
  receive the ball more [post 45434739, 2020-11-22, chucky06]. **opinion**
- DM: against Wings, DMs make more tackles than CBs [post 45681353, 2021-09-10,
  chucky06; post 45480895, 2021-01-11]. Suggested DM profile: PAS 9/10, BC 8+,
  TAC 7+, STA 9+, SPD 8+, PI as high as possible [post 45695449, 2021-09-29,
  k33n3y]. **opinion**
- Stamina: a player with 6–7 STA should play ≤ 30 min at top level, and 8-STA
  defenders "feel the pinch" late in each half [post 45518485, 2021-02-21,
  chucky06]. Minimum 8 for DEF/MID and 7 for FW, because substitutes go to the
  forwards [post 45517974, 2021-02-20, caiobos]. Stamina needs depend on the
  tactic: compact shapes mean less running [post 45596129, 2021-05-20,
  killerbeeez (NC)]. **consensus**
- GK: "The difference that one keeping ball creates for a keeper is a lot more
  than … one tackling ball … for a defender" [post 44569528, 2018-09-24,
  jedy22]. Exp counts more than secondaries [post 44564300, 2018-09-20, civilu].
  9GK/10PI/10EXP is "way better" than 10GK/0PI/1EXP [post 45308132,
  2020-07-09, darkline]. GK Passing is irrelevant once STA, PI, AP, BC and SPD
  reach 8 [post 44179433, 2017-11-27, evosa]. **consensus**
- Set plays and penalties: no skill explains penalty success. One taker improved
  SP 4→6, EXP 8→10 and SHO 2→8, yet scored less [post 46557925, 2026-08-18,
  civilu]. A 10-SP player scored no set-piece goals in a season
  [post 46465267, 2025-11-06, killerbeeez (NC)]. **consensus: SP effect unclear**

### Topic 12789668 "Specific Sim Notes/Improvements thread" (2021, read in full)
No Crew or staff posts in this thread: 12 posts, and the main author dagriggstar
has the RA role (a community assistant, not Crew). So it is **opinion**, from
an experienced observer.
- Stamina scales every skill. "a player with 10 shooting and 0 stamina by the
  end of the game plays like a 'fresh' 0 shooting player" [post 45543547,
  2021-03-19, dagriggstar (RA)]. This supports STA as a multiplier on other
  skills over a match, not just one additive skill. **opinion**
- Shot location and nearby defenders do not seem to affect shot success, so a
  very high defensive line is best [post 45543547]. Keepers save most 1-on-1s
  [same post]. **opinion**. This matches the round-1 view that GK Keeping is
  dominant.
- There is little difference in accuracy between ground and aerial passes, which
  is one reason Wings beats Short [post 45543547]. **opinion**
- Passive pressing "does nothing … defenders just don't do anything"
  [post 45543547]. **opinion**. This supports keeping Passive modifiers small.
- The pitch is so small that "Players are either attackers or defenders", with
  almost no working midfield [post 45543547]. **opinion**
- "The skill gap between each ball level is too small" [post 45543547]. Each
  extra ball adds about 3% success per action, and 2 extra balls everywhere give
  only ~66:33 odds. The author says this is not based on data [post 45550318,
  2021-03-26]. **opinion**. This supports a flat `shape()` and modest weights.
- Heading should mean winning aerial balls, but currently only sets header
  accuracy [post 45550318]. This is consistent with the low defender Heading
  above.
- Suspected hidden factors: confidence (streaks), pressure from being the
  favourite, weather [post 45543547]. **opinion**
- Sim version: "last sim update was 2016 and we are using SIM 2.40"
  [post 45550527, 2021-03-26, numchacar]. **opinion, unverified**. It is the only
  hint of a post-2011 engine update; An FTS search on "2.40" finds nothing to support it; "2.4d" there is the
  MZManager tool, so this is probably a mix-up.

## Changes to RESULTS.md

1. **Experience: raise E from 0.05 to ~0.20** (exp 0→10 = up to +20%), or model
   it as a penalty below 9 at senior level, e.g. `cond *= 1 - 0.04*max(0, 9-exp)`.
   Weight it a bit more for GK and ST. Evidence: posts 45403148, 45416523,
   46184247, 45416477 and the poll (Exp ranks 4–5 everywhere). This overrides
   the 2011 "Exp reduced" Crew note, since a decade of current-engine play
   disagrees. Keep E separate so it can be calibrated.
2. **Form: raise F from 0.10 to ~0.20, applied mostly below 9**, e.g.
   `1 - 0.10*max(0, 9-form)`. Posts 45818363, 46343277.
3. **PI up for GK, CD, FB, CM (+DM). Do not raise it for W/ST.**
   - GK: PI .10→.12, STA .10→.12, SPD .08→.05, BC .10→.07, AP .05→.07
     (poll: STA ≈ PI > AP > BC ≈ SPD).
   - CD: PI .15→.18, HEA .05→.02, SPD .17→.15, BC .10→.10, plus .02 to STA
     (STA .17).
   - CM: PI .18→.20, BC .15→.17, PAS .20→.16, SPD .12→.10, SHO .05→.06,
     STA .14→.15 (poll: PI > BC > STA > PAS). The Short modifier still lifts PAS.
4. **Add DM as its own vector** (the poll treats it as separate, and DMs
   out-tackle CBs against Wings): TAC .20, PI .17, STA .14, SPD .13, PAS .12,
   BC .10, AP .08, HEA .02, SHO .04.
5. **Speed: add a soft cap**, e.g. `shape_spd` flat above 8, and lower ST SPD
   from .17 to .13 (adding to BC/PI). Keep W SPD at .20 (poll #1). Posts
   44530973, 46335339, 46335995.
6. **Wings modifiers**: CD `HEA×1.3` → `HEA×1.05` (defender heading barely
   matters). Add CD/FB `TAC×1.1, PI×1.1` and DM `TAC×1.2` under the
   opponent's Wings. Keep W `AP×1.4` and ST `HEA×1.6`, and consider `HEA×1.8`
   for the central striker, since one AP winger plus one heading CF carries the
   style. Posts 44093498, 45100870, 46224015, 45839036.
7. **Long balls: shrink all modifiers toward 1** (e.g. GK AP×1.5→1.2,
   CD AP×1.6→1.25, FB AP×1.4→1.2, ST SPD×1.2→1.05). Posts 46566556, 45798913,
   45798774.
8. **Stamina as a fatigue multiplier** (post 45543547: skills fade with stamina), plus **stamina thresholds**: an extra penalty for STA < 8 in DEF/MID and < 7 in
   FW (a "90-minute availability" factor), kept as is under Committed. Posts
   45518485, 45517974, 45303193.
9. **Optional "total balls" term** (hypothesis to test at calibration): blend in
   about 5–10% of the player's mean over all outfield skills. This is untested
   but a widely held belief. Posts 44155393, 45983977, 46237059.
10. **Shooting**: keep ST SHO but mark it contested. Data suggest a weak
    individual effect: topic 12892951, post 46403450.
11. **Set plays**: keep `setPieceScore` low-confidence, because there is no
    evidence that SP drives penalty success (posts 46557925, 46465267).
12. **Next steps / calibration**: use the detailed match stats live since
    2024-11-25 (passes, failed passes, tackles, assists, saves, distance, speed).
    Add a note on Wings/Short balance changing by 2026 (contested).
