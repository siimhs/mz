
B03: Youth Leagues
Introduction

Youth Leagues offer competitive league formats outside of the Senior League, and provide a valuable venue for developing young players. Currently, three Youth Leagues are available - the U18 League, U21 League and U23 League. In these Leagues teams compete in divisions of 12, playing each opponent both at home and away during the course of a 22-game season. Four League seasons are played each calendar year.

ManagerZone runs Youth Leagues in all countries. While many countries have their own Leagues, some countries are grouped together to ensure that all Leagues are active.

Season Tickets

To participate in an Extra League you will need a season ticket. Season tickets can be bought in the Youth League page (Club Members get a discount). After a ticket is purchased, you can opt in/out of all Leagues for the next season (one season ticket grants entry to the U18, U21 and the U23 League for one season).

Once you have entered a League, you will stay in that League until you opt out or run out of season tickets.

Season Day 2 is the last day to sign up for all Youth Leagues.

Three points are awarded for a win, one point for a draw and no points for a defeat. When two teams finish the season on the same number of points, their positions are calculated using a number of "tie breakers" in the following order of importance: goal difference, goals scored, most victories, results between the two tied teams, random draw.

Promotion and Relegation

Promotion and relegation works slightly different in the Youth Leagues compared to the Senior League. There are no playoffs in the Youth Leagues. Only the winner of each division is guaranteed promotion, but more teams have a chance to advance, depending on how many teams sign up for the league next season.

In the same way four teams risk relegation, but could in some cases remain in their present division.

League Schedule Screen

View the matches, both completed and upcoming, played by all the teams in your division. 
Top Scorers Screen

View the current season's top goal scorers (to view only your team's scorers, click the MY TEAM button at the top of the screen). In addition to the place in the list, name of the player and team abbreviation, the table contain the following columns:

GPNumber of league games the players have participated in
GNumber of goals scored
YCNumber of yellow cards received
RCNumber of red cards received

All these four columns are sortable.
Injuries/Suspensions Screen

View all players due to miss future League games as a result of injury or suspension. A player that is suspended in a certain Extra League can still compete in other Leagues and Cups, however an injured player cannot compete in any competitions until he is fully recovered. 
Message Board

View messages from other managers in your division and type your own by using the text box at the bottom of the screen and clicking the POST button.
