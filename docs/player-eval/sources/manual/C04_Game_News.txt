
C04: Game News

Here you can review all the latest ManagerZone news, including forthcoming cup competition highlights and new game features. To view historical news items, select GAME NEWS ARCHIVE in the Game News sub-menu. This information can also be viewed on the [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=1393] Clubhouse Home screen. Note that specific Team News is only shown on the [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=1358] My Team and [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=1394] Office Home and Clubhouse Home screens.
