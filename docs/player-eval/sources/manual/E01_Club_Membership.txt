
E01: Club Membership
Introduction

Club Members receive a range of extra services and features that greatly enhance the ManagerZone experience without providing them with a competitive advantage over non-members. For a full list of Club Member features, select CLUB MEMBERSHIP in the Store secondary menu.
Club Membership Subscriptions

Club Membership can be purchased for fixed periods. Members can see when their current Club Membership subscription expires by viewing the top of the Club Membership screen.

Fixed-Period Subscriptions

Club Membership can be purchased in 1-, 3-, 6-, 12- and 24-month subscription periods using Power Tokens (the longer the period, the cheaper the monthly price). To start a subscription, decide how many months you wish to become a Club Member for and click the appropriate button at the top of the screen.
