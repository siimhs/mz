
B11: World League
Introduction

The World League is a global tournament that brings the whole ManagerZone world together into a single contest. You will be able to compete in a Senior League and/or a U18 League exclusively against the best Clubs in the world. 

Teams that play in the Senior and/or the U18 World League will earn team money and experience. Whilst the team money earned will be much less than for the Senior League, it will help active Managers to support the cost of training and developing their team. 

The World League is very different from the Senior and Youth Leagues. There are three World League Editions per regular Season and matches are played every day.

World League Editions

A World League Editions will last around 30 days, which includes the match days, promotions/relegation and signup period. The World League Editions will NOT cross the regular Season boundary. 

Kick-off times will vary from Edition to Edition, to spread out the matches across the different time zones. 

Form

Influence on Form will be similar to the Youth Leagues: team form will not be punished by competing in the World League. 

Cards

The World League is a separate competition so cards will not affect other competitions.

For each Home Match played in the World League and the U18 World League you will earn Team Money. You can earn additional Team Money if you finish as one of the top four in your division (see below; amounts are displayed in EUR):

Promotion and Relegation

Promotion and relegation works slightly different in the World League compared to the Senior League. There are no playoffs in the World Leagues. Only the winner of each division is guaranteed promotion, but more teams have a chance to advance, depending on how many teams sign up for the league next Edition.

In the same way four teams risk relegation, but could in some cases remain in their present division.
