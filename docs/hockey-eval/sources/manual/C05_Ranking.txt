
C05: Ranking

Every team in ManagerZone has a ranking in order to give managers an idea of how they are progressing in comparison to fellow bosses around the world. Rankings are calculated from points awarded for a variety of different achievements in the game over the previous eight weeks. You can view your team's current MZ Ranking on the [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=726] My Team screen (hover over the figure to see your points total).

The Ranking screen displays the top 100 teams in the game, including their point totals. This list can be customised by using the dropdowns in the top-right of the screen to sort by nationality and division. To view [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=744] National Team points and rankings, select NATIONAL RANKING in the Ranking sub-menu.
