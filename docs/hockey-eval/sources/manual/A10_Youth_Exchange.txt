
A10: Youth Exchange

Youth Exchange

Introduction

The Youth Exchange is a valuable tool for developing your Youth Academy throughout the season. This feature gives you the chance to scout new youth players and exchange them with youth players already on your team. Access the Youth Exchange by clicking YOUTH EXCHANGE on the left side menu on the Youth screen.

Evaluating a new youth player

Each week three new youth players will appear in your Youth Exchange, giving you the chance to add them to your team in place of current members of your Academy. A scout report is available for purchase (€872 in Team Money) to help pinpoint the talents of a new youth. This report gives an idea of which position the player will be good at (centre/forward/defender/goal keeper), but does not give any indications of potential maxings.

Exchanging youth players

Once you have begun to evaluate a new youth player, a decision must be made within the available window.

Only open the page if you are considering an exchange, because the countdown for that player will begin as soon as the Youth Exchange page is opened. A timer displayed in the upper right corner of the screen will show how much time is left to accept or reject the exchange. A responsible manager will allow sufficient time to review and complete the exchange within the window.

Once you have decided to initiate an exchange, the change will take place without delay. The new player will join your team (and the old player will leave) immediately.

Be careful when accepting an exchange, because the trade is irreversible once completed.

Exchange limits

You can exchange up to 26 youth players during a season. When considering an exchange late in a season, you must decide if the lost training time is worth the chance of gaining a potentially better youth player.

Club Members can make up missed opportunities on the Youth Exchange (you may catch up by three missed weeks), but are still limited to 26 exchanges in a season.
