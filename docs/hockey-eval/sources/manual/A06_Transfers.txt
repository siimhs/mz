
A06: Transfers

Introduction

On top of hiring youth players and training up your current squad, another way to strengthen your team is to bring brand new senior players in via the transfer market.

Transfer Market Restrictions

New managers cannot use the transfer market until they've been in their job for two weeks or more (Club Members are given instant access to the transfer market). They will be notified via the Team News feed (see "My Team Home Screen" in [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=726] My Team) when they are able to start buying and selling players. Clubs are restricted to having three foreign players (players born outside the country in which the club is based) in their squad. Managers cannot make a bid for a player that is greater than the value of their current squad.

When a new Manager transfer lists a Player, the option to show Training Graph will be forced to 'Yes'. When the Manager have played ManagerZone for 182 days, they will be able to choose whether or not to show Training Graph. This limitation does not apply to Club Members.

Selling a Player

To sell a player, click the SELL-icon on his profile (see "Icons and Buttons" in [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=727] Squad), input your asking price (this must be no more than eight times his value, which is also displayed on his profile), use the dropdown to choose how many days you wish to offer him for and click SELL. You will have to pay a fee of 1.25% of the asking price (the minimum and maximum allowable charges are detailed at the bottom of the screen), which will be charged whether or not your player is successfully sold.

You can view the progress of your sale on the Transfer Monitor screen. Note that, once you put a player on the transfer market, you cannot reverse your decision and if your asking price is met, he will be sold. If your player is sold, he will leave your club – and you will receive his transfer fee (minus the relevant sales fees and taxes) – 24 hours after the transfer deadline passes. Any players who remain unsold also return to your club at this point. You will be updated via the Team News feed. Note that you cannot sell [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=732] youth players.

Sales Taxes

As well as paying a fee to add players to the transfer market, you will also have to pay tax on any profits you make when you sell a player.

However, if you decide to sell a player soon after buying him, you will be penalised with higher taxes. You will also incur higher taxes if you sell a youth player within three years of him graduating to your senior team.

The tax rates on own products are calculated on the birth season of the player. The tax rate is calculated when the player joins the new club.

Buying a Player

Searching for New Players

To search for a new player, input your requirements (e.g. Nationality, Age and preferred attributes) in the form at the top of the Transfer Market screen and click the SEARCH-button. The resulting list of matching players displays each one's profile information as well as their club's asking price, bids that have already been made and the transfer deadline. To follow a player's progress without making a bid of your own, click the SHORTLIST-icon on his profile. This adds him to your [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=731] Shortlist, allowing you to quickly join the bidding at a later stage by clicking the PLACE BID-icon on his profile.

To avoid having to repeatedly input the same information, [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=758] Club Members can save custom searches by clicking MY SEARCHES in the Transfer Market sub-menu, clicking the CREATE SEARCH button, naming their search, inputting their requirements and clicking SAVE. To use the saved search, all they need to do is select it in the My Searches dropdown of the Transfer Market screen form and click SEARCH.

Bidding for a Player

The MangerZone transfer market is based on an auction system where the largest bid entered before the deadline purchases the player. To bid for a player, click the PLACE BID-icon on his profile and type your offer into the box, which is already pre-populated with the minimum amount you can offer (as a result of either the initial asking price or subsequent bids). Once entered, an indication of your target's potential salary based on your offer is shown at the bottom of the window. If you're happy with your offer and the player's salary, click BID. You can view the progress of your bid on the Transfer Monitor screen (see below). Note that once you make a bid, you cannot reverse your decision and if you win the auction, you will have to pay the money offered.

If a team bids for a player within three minutes of the transfer deadline, it is extended by three minutes in order to allow other managers to lodge counter bids. This process continues until there are no more late bids. When the transfer deadline passes, the team that has made the biggest offer purchases the player. To find out who tabled the highest bid, see the Transfer Monitor screen. The team who won the auction is shown at the top of the Bid History section.

Your player will join your team – and become available for [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=729] training and [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=728] selection – around 24 hours after the deadline has passed. You will be updated via the Team News feed (see “My Team Home Screen” in [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=726] My Team). At this point, the transfer fee for the player will also be taken from your funds. Until this time, his previous team can continue to play him in their matches.

Transfer Monitor Screen

Here you can review all your team's ins and outs, including previous transfers, [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=732] youth intake and players you've released. There's also a summary of all your dealings at the top of the screen, including your most expensive purchases and sales.

Transfer Rules Screen

In order to keep ManagerZone a competitive and enjoyable environment to play in, we have to lay down some basic rules for players to abide by. Transfer market-related rules are shown in point 4 of the Transfer Rules screen. These should be read before any manager buys or sells their first player, as not abiding by them can result in severe sanctions.

Any transfer that arouses suspicion, especially bids for players that are well over their value, will be checked by a Game Assistant (see "ManagerZone Assistants" in [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=756] Community) and, while this check is ongoing, the player's profile will display an exclamation mark. Until the Assistant is happy that the transfer is legal, the player will not be allowed to join his new club.

Bidding and Team Value

There are two rules to bidding on the market. First, any team can make a bid of at least €100.000 regardless of their team value. Second, teams with a team value over €2.700.000 can bid any amount they wish.

Foreign limits

There are limitations for how many non domestic players your team can have at any given time. Based on how busy the transfer market is in your country it belongs to a region, deciding how many foreign players you can possess.

Region A

Teams in Region A may have 10 foreign Players and 5 foreign Player with a Work Permit, for a total of 15 foreign Players.

Region B

Teams in Region B may have 11 foreign Players and 7 foreign Player with a Work Permit, for a total of 18 foreign Players. The foreign Player limits will be reviewed periodically, but will only be altered if we see that the transfer market for a country changes significantly.

Transfer Regions

Managers in countries with larger transfer markets generally have access to more domestic Players and Managers in countries with small transfer markets generally have fewer opportunities to buy domestic Players.

Each country in ManagerZone is assigned to a Transfer Region. The Transfer Regions were determined by:

Analysing the total activity in the transfer market.

Analysing how many domestic players were bought by teams.

Analysing how many foreign players were bought by teams.
