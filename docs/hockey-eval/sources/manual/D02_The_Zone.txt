
D02: The Zone

Introduction

The Zone is a magazine produced by managers for managers, which covers all the latest events at ManagerZone. When a new version of your local magazine has been published, the Z icon will light up in the Shortcut Bar at the top-right of the screen (see [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=721] Navigating ManagerZone). To view it, either click the icon or select THE ZONE in the Community menu. Then scroll down the screen to find your country and click the VIEW button. To view previous publications, click THE ZONE ARCHIVE in the The Zone sub-menu, find the country you are interested in, select a publication date from the dropdown and click VIEW.

Contributing to The Zone

Each country's The Zone is run by Reporter Assistants (RA), who provide a valuable service creating, editing and releasing each publication. As well as producing their own content, they also accept contributions from the Community. To submit your own article, send it to one of your country's RA's (see “ManagerZone Assistants” in [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=756] Community for more information) via their ManagerZone e-mail address (which can be found on their [link:./?p=manual_faq&cat_id=753] Profile). You can also talk to them (via e-mail or using their Guestbook) if you want to contribute to the magazine on a more regular basis or become an RA yourself.
