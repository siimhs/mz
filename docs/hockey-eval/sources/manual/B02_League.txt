
B02: League

Introduction

League games provide the core of a manager's competitive experience in ManagerZone. They are played every Wednesday and Sunday. Each club competes in a division of 12 teams, playing each opponent home and away during the course of a 22-game season. With season breaks lasting two weeks each, four League seasons are played in one calendar year.

ManagerZone runs Leagues in countries all over the world. Each has one top division containing the best 12 teams in that country and a "pyramid" system of divisions below it (for instance there are three div1's, nine div2's, 27 div3's etc.). Each division is identified by a code which first describes its level in the pyramid and then its number within that level (e.g. div4.53).

Table Screen

You can see your team's current position in your division in the Table screen, as well as their recently-completed and upcoming matches.

In the league table the following columns are presented, in addition to a team's position and name:

M | Matches played |

W | Wins |

D | Draws |

L | Losses |

+ | Goals scored |

- | Goals conceded |

= | Goal difference |

P | Points |

Last 6 | The results in the last six league games (green = win, yellow = draw, red = loss) |

Three points are awarded for a win, one point for a draw and no points for a defeat. When two teams finish the season on the same number of points, their positions are calculated using a number of "tie breakers" in the following order of importance: goal difference, goals scored, most victories, results between the two tied teams, random draw.

Promotion and Relegation

The team that finishes top of a division at the end of each season is promoted to a division in the level above, while the bottom three clubs are relegated to the one below (unless they already occupy a division at the bottom of the pyramid, in which case they remain in the same division). Teams that finish in second and third place play-off for promotion while those that finish in eighth and ninth play-off to stay in the division.

Inactive teams are not promoted, new Managers must play 1 League Match (non Play-Off Match) with their team in order to be promoted

Play-Offs

Play-offs consist of a four-way round-robin tournament to decide who is promoted and relegated. Each play-off contains one team that finished in the relegation play-off places of their division and three teams who reached promotion play-off places in the level below.

Each team in a play-off plays three matches – one against each of the others. If the team from the higher division finishes top of the play-off table, all the teams remain in the same divisions. However, if one of the lower-division teams comes first, they are promoted and the higher-division team is relegated.

Senior League prize money

Team money is awarded to the three best placed teams in every senior league. In the table below the amounts in Euros are presented for each division and place:

League Schedule Screen

View the matches, both completed and upcoming, played by all the teams in your division.

Top Scorers Screen

View the current season's top goal scorers (to view only your team's scorers, click the MY TEAM button at the top of the screen). In addition to the place in the list, name of the player and team abbreviation, the table contain the following columns:

GP | Number of league games the players have participated in |

G | Number of goals scored |

A | Number of assists produced |

P | Number of points (goals + assists) |

+/- | Number of goals scored minus number of goals conceded while the player was on the ice |

PIM | Number of penalty minutes |

FOL | Number of face-offs lost |

FOW | Number of face-offs won |

S | Number of shots the player has taken |

Sft | Number of shifts |

TOI | The player's time on the ice in minutes |

All these 11 columns are sortable.

Message Board

View messages from other managers in your division and type your own by using the text box at the bottom of the screen and clicking the POST button.

Division History

Select LAST SEASON TABLE in the League sub-menu to view how the current division looked at the end of the last campaign, including which teams were promoted and relegated to and from it. For further information on how all the play-offs finished, click PREVIOUS SEASON QUALIFICATIONS.

Walk Overs

Home teams that lose due to walk-over in a League match or any Cup match will now not earn any team money for that match.
