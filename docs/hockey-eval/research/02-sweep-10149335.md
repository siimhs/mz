# Sweep C: topics 10149335 … 11255601 (first posts 2012-07-27 … 2014-07-08)

298 titles scanned (forums 138 and 140). Era tag used below: **[old-sim]** is the
single hockey match engine that posters in 2013 described as unchanged for
years (see Engine versions). All claims in this range are from that engine; no
sim release happened in this window. Posts dated after 2014-07 come from long
threads in this range and carry the same tag unless noted.

## Summary (≤15 bullets, the answer)

1. **Positions**: one line has **C, LW, RW, LD, RD** plus G. Posters treat LW/RW
   and LD/RD as identical, so in practice 4 roles: **G, D, C, W** [post 39091424; post 41123132]. Nobody
   reports any effect of a player's "Shoots: Left/Right" field.
2. **Power is the top skater skill**, by a wide margin (consensus, [old-sim]). Rough
   thresholds: below 7 is poor, 8 is the minimum, top teams want 9–10, and 6 means sell [posts 38269227, 41430021,
   41431275, 38662837]. Swap tests: 9 Pow/6 Sho/6 Chk beats 6/9/9; 10 Pow/7 Sho beats 7/10; D 9 Pow/8 Chk
   beats 8/9; C 10 Pow/7 Chk beats 9/8; but **9 Pow/7 Sta beats 10 Pow/6 Sta** [39593548, 38269227, 41431683].
3. **Per-position rankings** (consensus of oilercountry, mattsadler, agapitod (GPA), mihairo, smiller29):
   - **C**: Pow > Sho ≈ Chk > Sta > Qui > Ska > PC (for face-offs). The C is the best all-round player on the line.
   - **W**: Pow > Sho > Qui > Sta > Ska (Chk useful). Like a C, but can be weaker.
   - **D**: Pow > Chk > Sta > Qui > Ska (some Sho/PI).
   - **G**: Keeping >> Sta > PI / Qui > PC/Pas. Power and skating do not matter.
4. **Passing and PI**: the majority view is that the sim ignores them for skaters
   (poolboy, deleted_5240478, gubsoul, mattsadler). This is **contested** by mihairo
   (2014), who says PI improves movement and saves stamina, and that Passing+PC give pass accuracy,
   with the formation deciding who needs it. Puck Control is a low-priority skater skill except for face-offs.
5. **Skating vs Quickness**: skating mainly helps a player **avoid or withstand body checks** and stay on his feet.
   Some veterans rank skating above quickness for all skaters and about equal for wingers [39601381, 41428309].
6. **Face-offs**: posters remember a hockey-only Crew update that made face-offs
   skill-based. Crew said more than one skill is involved, the "boost attributes" count, and
   **Power does not** [39672545]. Poster data: PC alone is weak. Two small samples
   give checking a correlation of 0.81 vs PC 0.48 and PI 0.47 [39810848] (opinion, tiny n).
7. **Penalties**: committed pressing and an "aggressive" line setting cause more penalties.
   **Higher Checking means fewer penalties**, which is why D take fewer than F (malkin78, toldan, poolboy).
8. **Tactic screen** (Order/Tactics editor): 4 regular lines plus PP1/PP2 and box-play
   lines. You drag players to place them on a rink diagram, and each line has a **commitment/aggressiveness** and
   **mentality** dropdown [40140160, 39494433]. Pressing = Committed means instant
   reckless checking, quick puck recovery, and breakaways given up. Nobody knows whether mentality does anything [38638562].
   Players think placement matters less than skills [40989619, 42813239].
9. **Lines and ice time**: L1 gets more ice time than L2, L2 more than L3, and L3 more than L4. The rotation depends
   on **stamina**. Estimates: L1 ~21, L2 ~18, L3 ~13, L4 ~8 min [43723208]. With high-stamina top lines:
   L1 20+, L4 5–10. With equal stamina, ~15 each [41437529]. Stamina is a "main skill" for every
   skater because it buys top-line ice time. Fill all 20 regular slots, or players tire [40812581].
10. **Line synergy** (opinion, prednibe, Swedish league winner): pair complementary D and
    linemates. You can win with D at quickness 5 [39664830, 39670667].
11. **Form**: updated on Tuesdays. Ideal week: 2 league + 4 friendlies (2 home, 2 away). Cup, friendly
    league and youth matches can only raise form. Overplaying lowers it. Form 10 is rare [39385340,
    39445166, 40358859, 39160608].
12. **Per-player match stats**: goals, assists, points, shots, F/O won/lost (and %), +/−, shifts,
    playing time. Team "Match Facts" list goals, shots, F/O won and %, saves, penalties and (by 2014) scoring chances.
    MZ's **+/− counts PP/PK goals and goalies** too. A shift-count bug shows up in OT. There are no stats for checks
    or interceptions [38010393, 39611126, 40250778, 40804141, 40859917].
13. **Numbers and tools**: nav's (2013) attacker/defender multipliers are the only
    numeric formula. See Findings §Tools. Other tools: demerzone (mz.demerzone.com, which
    moved to demzane.com in 2014, a "hockey evaluator"), mzplayer.se and a "mz spread sheet".
14. **Staff in range**: patrick7777, thunderexpress, powdersnow and mikelony posted only
    greetings and admin notes. **wholle** (no role in the DB) is staff: he announced Training 2.0 for season 40. bengtandersson
    relays news but says he is "not an employee". None of them said anything about the sim's skill use.
15. Examples of a strong player's skills: an England U21 NT average (2014): Pow 8.5, Sta 6.65, Chk 6.55,
    Sho 6.05, Qui 5.75, Ska 5.15, PC 3, Pas 2, PI 1.6 [40854435]. Ireland's U21 average is similar [40855823].

## Findings

### Positions and skill priorities
- Line = 2 D, 1 C, 2 W. Posters call them C, LW, RW, LD, RD and G, and speak of 21 players per squad
  (consensus) [post 39091424, 2013-04-08, nero; post 41123132, 2014-05-31, mihairo].
- oilercountry's ranking "in order of importance" [post 39116195, 2013-04-12] (opinion, which
  malkin78 says "not everyone agrees with" [39160608]):
  C: Pow, Sho, Qui, Sta, PC (face-offs), Ska. W: Pow, Sho, Qui, Sta, Ska. D: Pow, Chk,
  Sta, Qui, Ska. G: Kee, Sta, PI, Qui, PC/Pas. Also "besides goalie, power is by far the most
  important thing for all positions." malkin78 adds that Checking belongs 3rd–5th for C [39126668].
- mattsadler [post 39593548, 2013-07-06]: G Kee > Sta > PI > Qui. Power is the most important outfield skill.
  "Don't bother with passing or PI unless you have maxed all other skills". C is "usually your
  best player": Pow, high Sho and Chk, then Sta, Qui, Ska, a bit of PC. W has the same profile as C but can be weaker. D: Pow,
  lots of Chk, Sta, Qui, Ska. (consensus with agapitod (GPA/HMA) [post 40801123, 2014-03-11]:
  W Pow, Sho, Sta, Ska, Qui (+Chk). D Pow, Chk, Qui, Sta, Ska (+Sho). G Kee, Sta, PI.)
- Minimum 7 in key skills. G: Kee/Pow/PI/Qui. C: everything plus Sho and Chk, "MVP" [post 39598724,
  2013-07-07, deleted_5240478]. Rebuttal: "keepers don't need power. Stamina is far more important to a keeper
  than power" [39598764, 39599387, mattsadler] (consensus against GK power).
- Checking matters for forwards (consensus: poolboy, welshkamikaze, mattsadler) [topic 10734763,
  2013-07]. A C needs at least 7 checking [39569964]. For D, stamina comes 3rd after power and checking/shooting [39580982].
- Wingers vs centres: a player with 9 Pow, 9 Qui, 9 Sho, 4 Chk is "a winger, he will never be anything else". With
  9–10 checking he could become a centre [posts 41221729, 41222186, 2014-06-30] (consensus). So **checking
  separates C from W**.
- **Power as multiplier**: "Power is the be all and end all", which is "kinda" a multiplier for other skills
  [posts 39667281, 39670403, 2013-07-17, deleted_5240478] (opinion). mihairo [41428309, 2014-08-26]:
  Power "doesn't necessarily do anything by itself". It works in combinations: Pow+Chk gives a knock-down body
  check, Pow+Qui lets a winger force entry at the blue line, Pow+Sho gives a power shot from the blue line (important for C) (opinion).
- Shooting for D: D shoot from the point if you win draws [39602803, prednibe]. Check your stats to see
  whether your D actually shoot. Some players would put PI ("if it is a skill multiplier") ahead of D shooting [39602679,
  madmonk] (opinion).
- Goalkeeper: 10 Keeping is a must for top leagues, then stamina, quickness and PI [39412813]. Training order Kee,
  Sta, Qui, PI, PC, then Ska [39650433, chowders, +1 deleted_5240478]. Skating is irrelevant because a goalie barely
  skates and cannot be checked [39413204, poolboy]. "Puck control and/or passing is great for IRL-keepers not
  MZ-keepers" [39644435, prednibe]. But a goalie with passing "becomes more offensive minded" [40432757, shroomy] and passes out
  instead of holding the puck, which avoids defensive-zone face-offs [41431786, mihairo] (contested). 10 Keeping/7 Sta is preferred
  over 9/10 [topic 10314415]. 10 Keeping is far better than 8, and 8 Keeping at age 24 is "useless" [topic 11169118] (consensus).

### Skating, quickness, stamina
- Skating keeps a player on his feet when checked and gives more lateral movement to avoid hits
  [37563640, 2012-08, shroomy]. It also helps dodge rivals with the puck [38591686, toldan]. "No point being quick if
  you can't stay on your skates" [39622804]. Contested: omarthesilk thinks skating is speed and quickness is
  evasion [38593036]. Quickness may also govern reactions to shots and loose pucks [39018751] (opinion).
- Skating is the only counter to high power: players with 7–8 Pow "need +8 skating" [41431683, mihairo] (opinion).
- Stamina is a "main skill" (mattsadler [39671402, 40854196]; mihairo). Players with 6 stamina can still
  be useful on L4 [41431275].

### Tactics
- The Order/Tactics editor sets the "aggressiveness of each line" [40140160, 2013-10-18, poolboy]. It has two
  dropdowns, 'commitment' and 'mentality' (poolboy's labels, from memory) [39494433]. "With the aggressive
  mode you make more penalties, use normal instead" [39506323, toldan]. → Per-line aggression exists.
- Pressing: Committed makes every player without the puck go at the carrier at once with "reckless checking".
  You win the puck back fast, but a missed check gives up breakaways and 2-on-1s. "I do not know if Mentality
  does anything" [38638562, 2013-01-29, malkin78] (opinion).
- Placement: you drag players on a rink diagram per line, including PP and box play [40982078, 42813247].
  Players report little effect: "player skills have a bigger effect than position" [40989619, halil, 2014-04].
  "Not like football where tactics rule all" [42813239, smiller29, 2015-12] (consensus that it is weak).
  Ideas: wingers high ("cherry picking") with the C slightly back, or quick D up at the dots (slow D get burned) [42813239].
  mihairo describes "2-1-2" and "2-2-1" set-ups: in 2-1-2, D and C need passing+PC; in 2-2-1, W need it; C is a
  "tank" [41431683, 2014-08] (opinion).
- Contrast: prednibe (2013) said there are "a lot of tactical ideas that actually [have] a great impact" in
  hockey [39311233] (contested).
- PP: attacking players (good shooting). Box play/PK: good checking [43723208]. A player who sits only on special lines plays
  only when there are penalties [40811091, agapitod].
- External guides cited in 2012 (unreachable for us): Triquetra's site `users.telenet.be/MZ/Triquetra/indeling.htm`
  (dead by 2013) and `freewebs.com/mzhockeyguide/tactics.htm` [37572944].

### Lines and ice time
- L1 plays more than L2, L2 more than L3, L3 more than L4, so put your best 5 on L1 [40864506, 2014-03]. You can play only 3 lines if they have high stamina [39547003].
- mihairo's stamina-driven rotation [41428309, 41437529, 2014-08]: with ≥8 stamina on L1, the order runs L1-L2-L3-L1-L2-L3-L4.
  Stamina-heavy top lines get L1 20+, L2 15–20, L3 10–15 and L4 5–10 min. Equal stamina 7 gives about 15 min each. The stronger lines
  can get "twice as many scoring chances" (opinion, no data).
- smiller29: L1 21, L2 18, L3 13, L4 8 min. It changes "drastically" if L4 has excellent stamina [43723208,
  2017-02] (opinion). Players cannot set ice time by hand [41436136].
- There are 20 regular slots. Using fewer players makes them tire faster than the opponent [40812581].
- For calibration: shifts and playing time per player are on the match stats page [topic 10738967].

### Face-offs
- prednibe [39672545, 2013-07-18]: face-offs "were random until Crew somehow implemented an update just for hockey".
  The news then said that more than one skill is involved, that the boost attributes (PC, PI, Passing?) count, and that
  **Power is not involved**. The source is memory of a news item (prednibe calls it official, unverified).
- Common belief: PC (toldan says "the only skill that matters" [39776470], plus agapitod and chowders), then PI, then checking.
- Data: malkin78 [39809636]: 59% (6.2 PC, 8 Chk), 52% (3.9 PC, 6 Chk), 50% (7.0 PC, 8 Chk), 45% (2.7 PC, 7 Chk), all PI 1–3.
  krunkymunky [39810848]: 36% (3 PI, 2 PC, 7 Chk), 41% (10 PI, 1 PC, 7 Chk), 52.5% (8 PI, 2 PC, 8 Chk), 50.5% (7 PI, 3 PC, 9 Chk).
  On the pooled 8 centres, correlation is **Chk 0.81, PC 0.48, PI 0.47**. oilercountry: 72% for a C with 3 PC, 9 Chk, 6 PI
  [39797616]. The team that wins the game usually wins most face-offs [39798103]. (opinion, n tiny)
- Shroomy: one maxed player's F/O% rose each season as his PI went from 3 to 7 [37909729].
- Face-off wins do not guarantee results: 90% F/O and 22 shots ended 1:0, 73% F/O ended 0:9 [37543170].

### Penalties
- Checking reduces penalties [39360125, 39507560, malkin78]. Aggressive/pressing settings increase them
  [39494433, 39506323]. PC+Pow+Chk gives a "clean bodycheck (without penalty)" [41428309, mihairo] (opinion).
- Early penalties change the line order and swing games [38266844, underdog74] (opinion).

### Form, age and other factors
- Form updates on Tuesday morning, and Monday friendlies count for the previous week [39329461]. Ideal week: 2 league + 4
  friendlies, 2 of them home and 2 away (consensus: mattsadler, agapitod, amaaat [39445166]). Cup, friendly league and youth matches can only help form
  [39160608, 39521098]. Form 10 appears when team form is very high, about 9.5 [38946326].
- Older players are "better for the sim" than younger ones of the same value. Home advantage. Selling players may
  hurt (romanasu [37877390], opinion). Experience is never discussed in this range.
- Player value: Keeping adds the most value [41213204]. Per mihairo, PC, Sho and Chk come next for skaters [40907665] (opinion).

### Tools and formulas
- nav's rating system [post 39852579, 2013-08-21] (opinion; "near 200 points is great"):
  - ATTACKER: PI×3, Pow×5, Ska×3, Pas×2.5, Qui×3.5, Sho×5, PC×1.5, Chk×2.5, Sta×3.5
  - DEFENDER: PI×2, Pow×5, Ska×3, Pas×2, Qui×3.5, Sho×3, PC×1, Chk×5, Sta×3.5
  - Replies: passing is too high [39853305]. "Ignore passing and PI completely. Power should be the highest
    multiplier" [39857632].
- demerzone (`mz.demerzone.com`), cited as a hockey player evaluator [topic 10169441]. It moved to `demzane.com`
  in 2014-01 (by "Demerzel, DZ Blue") [40557138]. We cannot reach it (no web).
- The squad stats page shows the team's average skills [40819138]. A cup stats page was added in 2014-05 [topic 11205484].

### Per-player match statistics
- +/−: +1 if on ice for a goal for, −1 against. **In MZ it includes PP/PK goals and goalies** (unlike the NHL).
  This is why good PK defenders show a worse +/− [38010393, malkin78; 40805193, agapitod].
- Match stats page: shifts and playing time per player [topic 10738967]. The shift count is wrong in OT, a cosmetic bug [39611126].
- Team Match Facts: Goals, Shots, Faceoffs Won, Faceoff Percent, Saves, Number of Penalties, plus scorers [40250778].
  Scoring chances appear by 2014-03 [40804141].
- The league table lists player stats including F/O Won and F/O Lost, sortable [39672545].
- No checks or interceptions are tracked, and nothing measures defenders [40859917, 40860250].

## Engine versions
- **Old sim, unchanged for years**: "Hockey has had the same sim for at least 4 years" [39667281, 2013-07-17].
  "Engine ... feels untouched" from 2002 to about 2011 [38515427, 2013-01-12]. "The main functions of the game ... are
  unchanged" [39305093, 2013-05-13]. Crew may feel "'the old sim' is too analyzed" [39311233].
- **Face-off update** (hockey only), from before 2013-07 with the date unknown. Face-offs became skill-based and power is not used
  [39672545]. Look for it in the earlier ranges or news.
- Non-sim changes in this window: training graphs can be shared on the transfer list (2012-08-09) [37465599].
  Retired players can become coaches (2012-09-06) [37652811]. **7 players per coach slot** (2013-01-14) [38529089]. National
  player star on the player page (2013-11) [topic 10957017]. Site upgrade (2013-11): only the first 2 home friendlies pay [40336552].
  New cup stats (2014-05). Financial reports (2014-06) [topic 11208019].
- **Training 2.0** from **season 40** (autumn 2013), announced by staff member wholle [39656600, 39915502; news nid 71617].
  Youths train much faster, players under 25 faster, and older players deteriorate more (about 3 pucks until retirement per prednibe [39677819]).
  It added new camps (ITC/YCC) and a planned "Club Physio" [39663496]. Training only, with no change to the sim.

## Changes to the model
- **Roles G, D, C, W**: merge LW/RW and LD/RD (consensus). No handedness factor.
- **Skater weights**: Power is the largest weight at every skater position (consensus, [old-sim]). Possible
  Power weights of about 1.5× Sho or Chk. The swap tests (9/6/6 > 6/9/9, and 10 Pow/7 > 7/10) imply Pow ≥ ~1.5× the main secondary skill.
  Stamina is close to Power: 9 Pow/7 Sta beats 10/6, so add a stamina weight of about Pow's (opinion).
- **C** = W profile + Checking (Chk high) + small PC (face-offs). **W**: Sho, Qui, Ska high, Chk low. **D**: Chk
  second, Sho low (consensus).
- **G**: Keeping dominant (10 ≫ 8), then Sta, then PI/Qui. Power and Skating weights ≈ 0 (consensus).
  Passing and PC are small (contested).
- **Passing and PI weights ≈ 0–small for skaters** (majority). PI may deserve a small weight (contested by mihairo, madmonk).
  PC small for skaters, except a face-off term for C.
- **Face-off term for C**: Checking + PC + PI, with no Power (prednibe's memory of Crew news, plus weak data in
  favour of Checking).
- **Penalty risk**: high Checking lowers it. Aggressive line setting or Committed pressing raises it. If tactic modifiers are modelled,
  Committed/aggressive should raise the Checking/Power/Stamina weights and add a penalty cost for low Checking (opinion).
- **Mentality**: no evidence of an effect. Leave it out or make it neutral.
- **Line/ice time**: weight a player by expected ice time: L1 ≈ 21, L2 ≈ 18, L3 ≈ 13, L4 ≈ 8 min (opinion, two
  posters). Top-line stamina raises its share. PP lines value Shooting, PK lines value Checking (opinion).
