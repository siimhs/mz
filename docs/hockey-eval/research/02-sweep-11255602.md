## Summary (≤15 bullets, the answer)

1. **Positions**: GK, LD, RD, LW, RW, C. A line is 1 C + 2 W + 2 D. The tactic has 21 numbered slots: L1–L4 plus PP1–2 and BP1–2, and the GK plays all 60 min. Slot #1 is the L1 centre. 10+1 is the minimum and 20+1 is recommended [post 43101911 floodish; 43327545 agapitod]. *consensus, 1.2*
2. **Power is the top skill for every skater**. Posters name it again and again, most strongly from 2014 to 2017 [43101911, 41431275, 44020574, 44027521, 44022618]. Top teams want Power ≥9, and 8 is the floor elsewhere [42844330, 42845426, 43867615]. *consensus, 1.1+1.2*
3. **Position profiles** (agapitod's Portuguese-forum list and floodish's guide agree):
   - GK: Keeping ≫ Stamina > PI > Quickness > Passing/PC/Skating.
   - D: Power, Checking > Stamina, Quickness, Skating > Shooting > PI/PC/Passing.
   - W: Power, Shooting > Stamina, Quickness, Skating > Checking > PI/PC/Passing.
   - C: Power, Shooting, Checking > Stamina, Skating, Quickness, PC > PI, Passing ("everything", most balanced).

   [43081052, 43101911, 43984644, 41675559, 42397309] *consensus (moderator opinions, no staff)*
4. **Stamina is second overall**: P8/C8/S8 beats P7/C10/S6 [41351092]. Fatigue follows a player's own time on ice, not the game clock [41402211]. agapitod thinks 1.2 plus the new deterioration made Stamina and Quickness matter more [43081052]. *consensus*
5. **Skating is the only counter to Power**: it helps a player avoid or absorb bodychecks, and low-Skating players "fall down". agapitod rates P8/Sk8 above P9/Sk4 [41428309 mihairo, 42230208, 42232505, 43558362, 44020574]. *consensus*
6. **PI / Passing / PC are contested**. In direct comparisons PI beats Passing [11861906: agapitod, floodish, smiller29, noild]. PC is the main faceoff skill, with PI and Quickness helping [42770521, 42770267]. Some say PI and Passing are "not used on the sim" [41430021], or that the three have "no distinct functions" and are only add-ons [44030444 prednibe]. Against that: a C with Passing 2 led his league in assists four times [42615221/42615773]. *contested*
7. **For GK, PI is the 3rd skill**: 8 PI/6 Q is preferred over 6 PI/9 Q (11691156). The training camps that include Keeping are Keeping/St/PI, Keeping/St/Q and Keeping/St/Pass [41535387]. Power, Shooting and Checking are useless for GK [43145069]. *consensus*
8. **Tactic options**: per line, **Mentality** (Attacking/Balanced/Defensive) and **Pressing** (Committed/Normal/Passive), plus where each player is dragged inside his zone box. Nothing mentions a passing style or aggression option, and hockey has no Test Tactic [43101911, 12159275]. *consensus*
9. **Tactic → skill shifts**:
   - Attacking: D join the attack and shoot (need Shooting, Quickness, Stamina).
   - Defensive: W track back and tackle (need Checking, Quickness, Stamina).
   - Committed: needs Stamina (L1 ≥8–9, L2 7–8, never 5–6), causes more penalties and more offensive-zone turnovers.
   - Recommended: Attacking on PP, Defensive on BP. PP takes shooters; BP/PK takes Power, Checking and Stamina (some use 4 D).

   [43154514, 43685875, 42397853, 41653144, 43723208] *opinion/consensus, no staff*
10. **Placement inside the box matters little**. The one consistent rule: fast D can play high, slow D should stay near the goal. Several posters saw no effect at all [43293695, 43301142, 43301641, 43685875]. *consensus, weak effect*
11. **Ice time / lines**:
    - 1.1 rule of thumb: L1 21, L2 18, L3 13, L4 8 min [42397309]. mihairo: high-Stamina L1/L2 get +20 / 15–20 / 10–15 / 5–10 min, and level Stamina gives about 15 min each [41437529].
    - After the "stamina/line change update" (1.2): the sim sends out the line with the most stamina left, so keep each line within 1–2 Stamina pucks [43672709, 43862687].
    - 4 lines beat 3 unless all 15 skaters have Stamina ≥8–9 [11856621 floodish, agapitod, smiller29, apolo72].

    *consensus*
12. **Official (news quoted by users, 2015-04)**: "The SIM recognize the partial skills and Players benefit from each part of the skill" [42212908]. Use fractional skill values if we have them. The new deterioration (2015-04-14) only hits Quickness and Stamina, from age 27–29, at most 2 pucks each [42488046, 44284931].
13. **Form matters** (9 is the target, at about 6 matches a week, updated on Tuesdays). There is no injury and no bench. agapitod does not believe there is a hidden Experience attribute. Player value depends on skills only [43101911, 42861103, 42519260, 43188810, 43061003]. *consensus/opinion*
14. **Per-player match stats** seen:
    - Each match: shots, goals, a player **"rate"** (league-round extremes from −2.34 to +18.92; hat-tricks give 15–19), Shifts, +/-, faceoff %, a "Special team" line.
    - Each season: G/A/P per player.
    - Sim 1.1 calibration: about 16 shots per team per match and 14–26 % of shots scored (Irish TL 2014) [11371014, 43849173, 42615221, 41927312].
15. **No numeric weights from any source.** Tools exist but no formulas were posted: DZMZHV31 (demzane.com), managerzone.eu tactich.php, and the FBB Blue Tactic Excel (mrblue74, user-set skill priorities). The randomness is large: 25–10 shots for a 0–1 loss, and in sim 1.1 the away team won 54 % against 36 % for home (3,828 top-league games) [11417765, 11600082, 12112871, 42149097].

In France: Power 8, Checking or Shooting 8, Stamina 7 [43860462]. smiller29's order: Power > Shooting/Checking > Stamina > Quickness > Skating [42848436]. *consensus on the order*
- mihairo's skill combinations [41428309, 2014, 1.1] (*opinion*):
  - Power + Checking: knock-down bodycheck.
  - Power + Quickness: W forced entry at the blue line.
  - Power + Shooting: C power shot from the blue line.
  - PC: precision. PC + Shooting means goals; PC + Checking means clean checks, i.e. fewer penalties.
  - PI: positioning, saves stamina.
  - Passing: pass speed; precision needs PC.
  - Shooting and Checking also stand for offensive and defensive understanding, so all skaters need both.

- Training camps have no Shooting/Stamina combination [41484913]. *fact about the camps*

## Changes to the model
1. Positions: **GK, D (LD/RD), W (LW/RW), C**. Treat L/R as the same role. There is no separate forward type beyond W and C. *consensus*
2. Skater base weights (proposed ordering, to be tuned):
   - D: Power 1.0, Checking 0.9, Stamina 0.7, Skating 0.6, Quickness 0.6, Shooting 0.35, PC 0.25, PI 0.25, Passing 0.1.
   - W: swap Checking and Shooting.
   - C: Power 1.0, Shooting 0.75, Checking 0.75, Stamina 0.7, Skating 0.6, Quickness 0.6, PC 0.5 (faceoffs), PI 0.35, Passing 0.15.

   Keeping counts 0 for skaters. *ordering is consensus; numbers are ours*
3. GK: Keeping 1.0 ≫ Stamina 0.5 > PI 0.4 > Quickness 0.3 > Passing 0.15 ≈ Skating 0.1 ≈ PC 0.1. Power, Shooting and Checking count 0. *consensus*
4. Interaction terms: the effect of Power should fall as the opponent's Skating rises (Power ↔ Skating), so it is worth adding a small bonus when both Power and Skating are high. Shooting × PC for scoring. *opinion (mihairo, smiller29, agapitod)*
5. Use fractional skills when they are known (*official*: partial skills count).
6. Tactic modifiers (per line):
   - Attacking: D Shooting weight up (+0.2), Quickness and Stamina up.
   - Defensive: W Checking weight up (+0.2), Quickness and Stamina up.
   - Committed: Stamina weight up (+0.2 for all skaters), plus a penalty risk.
   - Passive: Stamina weight down.
   - PP: Shooting up. BP: Checking and Power up.

   *opinion, consistent, no staff*
7. Placement: only D Quickness matters for a high-placed D. Skip it, or add a tiny term. *weak consensus*
8. Lines: weight a player by the expected share of ice time. In 1.2 the time evens out when line stamina is level, so a line-depth score (St ≥8 needed for 3 lines) matters more than the L1 weight. *consensus*
9. Form: multiply by a form factor. Experience: no evidence it affects anything, so leave it out. *opinion*
