# Sweep: topics 7848005 .. 10149335 (agent B, 2009-12 .. 2012-07)

Range: 298 topics in forums 138/140. One topic (7848005, Israel NT) goes back
to 2009-12-11. Every other topic starts 2011-03 or later. The DB holds only 34
hockey posts from before 2011-03, all in that one thread, so the **launch era is
almost missing from the local forum**. Era tag used here: **E2011** (the sim as it
stood 2011-03 .. 2012-07, possibly changed around 2010-11). Hockey had no Crew sim
release notes in this range.

## Summary (the answer)

1. **Positions in use: G, D, C, W** (the forum says "wingers", "forwards" or "attackers"). Lineups are 4 even-strength lines (3 also allowed) plus **Power Play 1–2 and Box Play 1–2** units. People usually field 20 skaters + 1 G. With no G the match is lost 5-0. *consensus, E2011.*
2. **D skills: Power, Checking first, then Stamina, Quickness, Skating.** Shooting helps on PP points. PI and Passing are disputed. *consensus.*
3. **W skills: Shooting, Power first, then Quickness, Skating, Stamina.** *consensus.*
4. **C = W skills + Checking + Puck Control.** PC wins faceoffs, and C is "the best player in the line". *consensus.*
5. **G skills: Keeping first, then Stamina, PI, Quickness, Passing**, with Skating and PC doubtful. Several NCs and GPAs say so. *consensus.*
6. **PI, Passing and PC were widely thought to do little in this sim** (yib: "no impact"). Exceptions: PC at faceoffs, and PI maybe for positioning and for D making bad passes. There is a single report (ramdu, 2011-12) that "passing can be useful" now. *consensus / contested.*
7. **NT selection thresholds (England 2011): D 9+ Che, 9+ Pow, 7+ Ska, 7+ Qui, 6+ Sta.** C 8+ Sho and Che, 9+ Pow, 7+ Sta/Qui/Ska. W 9+ Sho, 9+ Pow, 7+ Qui/Ska, 6+ Sta. G 10 Kee, 8 Sta. U21 minimums (from the Argentine forum, 2 to 3 pucks lower) are listed below. *opinion, but numeric.*
8. **Senior training-camp packages (game data)** only come as "Attacker" (4 kinds) and "Defender" (3 kinds) bundles, plus General and Condition. These are the game's own skill groupings (see Findings).
9. **Ice time:** line 1 plays most and 4 least. Lines change only at stoppages (goal, held shot, penalty, icing). A line holding a tired player, e.g. one who plays on two lines, goes out less often. Opponent lines and their stamina also shift ice time. *consensus.* Box placement on the tactic board showed **no visible effect** (several testers). Mentality (offensive/defensive) does have one. *contested.*
10. **Tactic options seen:** line placement boxes, Mentality, Pressing (Committed is used for attack). No "Passing style" or aggression option is ever named. Hockey has **no Test Tactic** (2016). Many penalties are linked to "playing aggressive", which drains stamina (opinion).
11. **Match-day training boost:** a player standing in a skill's training field gets an in-game boost even when maxed. A table relayed by a GPA (2024) gives **effective = 1 + 0.9·x** (0→1.0, 5→5.5, 9→9.1, 10→10). It is in the site changelog. *official-ish (MA/GPA citing changelog).*
12. **Per-player match stats:** GP, G, A, P, PIM, +/-, FOW, FOL, S, Sft (shifts), TOI. Team match facts: Goals, Shots, Faceoffs Won, Faceoff %, Saves, Penalties, Injuries (always 0). **No injuries exist in hockey.** *official (UI).*
13. **Sim history (weak):** 2010-04 "Crew updating a new SIM". By 2011-11 "changed goalie behaviour … faceoffs more stat-based, according to MZ" and "since they changed the sim up". Others say it was the "same old sim" from 2009 to 2012. In 2012-02 ramdu said Crew were "actually updating the game" and asked for wishes. *contested.*
14. **Third-party tools:** Demerzel's "MZ Hockey Value" (DZMZHV v2.2/v3/v3.1, 2011–2020; it builds a best lineup, but the formula is not in the DB), MZ Manager (rates defenders), a Danish xlsm rating sheet (2020), luckman's tactic generator (2014), and Methos/Triquetra's lineup guide (gone by 2013). Skill *value* note: hockey skills are valued at about half of football's, **except Keeping** (opinion).
15. **Form:** most top players sit at 9 and 10 is rare or random. About 4 friendlies + 2 league games a week keeps form up. An overplayed player recovers in about 1 week. Stamina is "most important" for late periods. A 10-keeping G with 0 stamina "might play like 5" at the end of a game (opinion).

## Changes to the model
- Use **4 positions: G, D, C, W**. No separate LW/RW (handedness is contested and minor). *consensus.*
- **D:** Checking ≈ Power > Stamina > Quickness ≈ Skating > Shooting (PP) > PI > Passing ≈ PC (≈0). *consensus (mattsadler, yib, ms82, toma22 thresholds).*
- **W:** Shooting ≈ Power > Quickness ≈ Skating > Stamina > Checking (small) > PI > PC, Passing ≈ 0. *consensus.*
- **C:** Power ≥ Shooting ≈ Checking > Quickness ≈ Skating ≈ Stamina > Puck Control (faceoffs; give it real weight, ≥ W's) > PI. *consensus on PC.*
- **G:** Keeping dominant (think ×5 the rest). Then Stamina > PI > Quickness > Passing > PC ≈ Skating. *consensus.*
- **Stamina** should scale the whole score: it decides late-game play and line ice time. Lines 1 and 2 need more, and the 4th line or PP/BP specialists less. *consensus.*
- **Line or slot effects:** weight line 1 > 2 > 3 > 4 when combining player scores into a team score. Players on PP need offence and PK (box) players need defence. The PK "front two" and the PP C need PC. *consensus/opinion.*
- **Tactic modifiers:** there is no forum evidence linking box placement to skills. Mentality might shift attack vs defence, and Committed pressing costs stamina (manual) and may bring more penalties. Keep tactic modifiers small. *contested.*
- **Optional match-day modifier:** a skill on the training field counts as 1 + 0.9·x. *official-ish.*
- Use NT thresholds as calibration anchors: an NT-level D ≈ Pow 9, Che 9, Ska/Qui 7, Sta 6+.
