## Summary (≤15 bullets, the answer)
1. **Power is the dominant skater skill, and staff said so.** Crew: "Power being by far the most important skill in Hockey. It is a flaw in the game that we (for now anyway) will have to live with" [post 44719547, 2019-02-13, powdersnow (Crew)]. Every outfield ranking in the range puts it first (2019–2026), and nobody reports a change after the 2020 fix. Cut-offs: under 8 Power is "disposable", 9+ preferred [45034762 floodish 2019; 46000490 lukaszsz182 2023; 46480133 scruttino 2025; 46569251 2026]. **official + consensus, E-pre and E-cur**
2. **Current outfield order** (scruttino, NC, 2025–26): Power > Stamina > Skating > Quickness > Shooting/Checking (by role) > Puck Control > Play Intelligence > Passing > Keeping. "Players with high Power, Stamina, Skating & Quickness are beasts even with lower Shooting or Checking" [46480133, 46481086]. **consensus, E-cur**
3. **Positions:** each line has 5 skaters, **C, LW, RW, LD, RD**, plus one GK who plays the whole match. The editor has Lines 1–4, PP1–2 and BP1–2 [43101911, floodish guide 2016, re-confirmed "no changes" 2020, 2022 and 2023]. Treat LW/RW as one "W" and LD/RD as one "D": stick hand is cosmetic [46569369]. **consensus**
4. **Role weights (GPA guide, still valid):** D: Power, Checking > Stamina, Skating, Quickness > Shooting, PC, PI. W: Power, Shooting > Stamina, Skating, Quickness > Checking, PC, PI. C: Power, Shooting, Checking > Stamina, Skating, Quickness, PC > PI, Passing. GK: Keeping > Stamina, PI > Quickness, Passing, PC [43101911]. **consensus (GPA)**
5. **Goalkeeper:** Keeping first; keep only keepers who reach 10. Then Stamina > PI > Quickness > Skating > PC > Passing. Power, Shooting and Checking are filler [46480133; 45504272]. **consensus**
6. **Face-offs depend on Puck Control.** Crew: "Puck Control is the most important skill to win FOs", and Power is not a factor [44384899, 44500455, 2018, mikelony (Crew)]. Others add PI [45229031, 46121417]. The centre usually takes the draw, but not always [46125038]. PC barely matters outside face-offs [46544034]. **official (soft "I'd say") + consensus**
7. **Passing is close to useless.** "Players pass the puck fine even with 0 pucks" [46481086, 46544527 scruttino]. PI helps a little: 8–10 PI cuts wasted chances "but not as much as you'd hope" [46493186]. **consensus, E-cur**
8. **Skating vs Quickness is contested.** malkin78 (2019) puts Quickness, Stamina and Shooting/Checking above Skating [44872579]. scruttino (2026) says "Skating is more important than quickness", with Quickness usually maxing at 6–8 [46489292]. propaans1337 (2026): low Skating means players fall over and can't change direction [46577446]. **contested**
9. **Stamina decides ice time.** When play stops, the line with the most stamina left goes on [44433686]. A low-stamina player lowers his line's average, so the line gets fewer shifts, and one line can stay out 7–8 minutes [45772159]. Tired players are said to play like they have fewer pucks [45768774]; one poster disagrees [45769016]. Put low-stamina players (≥4) on the least-used line [46489292]. Playing only 2 lines (10 skaters) gives "very noticeable" drops [46480668]. **consensus / partly contested**
10. **Tactic options that exist:** Mentality (Attacking/Balanced/Defensive) and Pressing (Committed/Normal/Passive), **set per line**, plus where each player sits on the board and who is in which line. Usual advice: Attacking on PP, Defensive on BP [43101911]. **No passing style, aggression, ice-time or rotation setting, no substitutions, no 6th skater or reserve GK, no test-tactic** (2018–2025 posts). **consensus**
11. **Tactic modifiers (GPA, 2016, never contradicted later):** Attacking mentality sends defenders forward to shoot, which raises D Shooting, Quickness and Stamina. Defensive mentality has wingers tracking back and tackling, which raises W Checking, Quickness and Stamina. Committed pressing needs Stamina and causes more fouls; Passive suits low-stamina teams [43154514 agapitod; 45636746; 46505949]. **consensus-ish (single GPA + agreement)**
12. **Engine history in range:**
    - 2019-11-28: new HTML tactics editor (layout only).
    - 2020-06-23: Crew confirms an away boost that grows with team strength.
    - 2020-10-06: away-boost fix goes live.
    - 2021-08-16: 3D viewer rebuilt as a replica of the old one.
    - 2025-08-25: brand-new 3D viewer (visual only).
    - 2026-04: potentials shown in the UI.
    Veteran view: only two sim fixes since 2005, the stamina/fitness bug and the away boost [45927289]. **official dates (Crew), opinion on the rest**
13. **Training a maxed skill adds a small in-match bonus.** A player on the training field plays the trained skill as about s + 0.1·(10−s), e.g. 8→8.2 and 9→9.1. Source: a 2015 table plus a changelog screenshot [46257091, 46257095, marcelit0 MA/GPA, 2024]. **official-ish (changelog)**
14. **Value and total pucks are poor quality proxies.** Outfielders are trained in Keeping to raise value, and Keeping does not make a skater better [45447781, 45448240, 46001752]. Experience is hidden in hockey [45850182]. Height and weight do nothing [45131022]. **consensus / opinion**
15. **Per-match stats:** per team: goals, shots, faceoffs won, faceoff %, saves, penalties [45651740]. Per player: goals, assists [45850477] and **+/-** (the "Star line" achievement is +10 in one match [45788837]). The manual covers the rest. No tool formula with weights was found: MZ Manager 2.4d gives per-position ratings ("Winger 8.45, Center 7.86") with no weights posted, and Demerzel's DZMZHV tool has no formula [45565193, 46016850, 45269976].

## Findings

### Skills: overall
- Crew 2019: Power is by far the most important skill, a known flaw [44719547, powdersnow (Crew)]. **official, E-pre**
- vicek 2020: "power is by far the most important skill, and some skills like passing and play intelligence has much less importance"; everyone trains the same way [45255879]. **consensus, E-pre**
- floodish (GPA) 2019: under 8 Power means a disposable player. Quickness and Stamina matter for everyone, Checking for D, Shooting for attackers [45034762]. malkin78 2019: Skating useful for all, centres should be balanced, "Power is king" [45046534]. **consensus, E-pre**
- malkin78 2019 cut-offs: Power ≥9. Then Stamina, Shooting/Checking and Quickness as high as possible. Stamina ≥6 (6 only on the 4th line), Quickness ≥7 (6 is fine with 10 Power), primary Shooting/Checking ≥9 (8 acceptable). 7 Skating is fine [44872579]. **opinion with numbers, E-pre**
- Since the fix (E-cur): nik2780 2021 [45507495], lukaszsz182 2023 (Power 8 minimum, 9 preferred; C = everything W and D have) [46000490], her1991 2023 [46000561] and shenaty43 2026 ("9 or 10 Power, everything else sucks") [46569251] all agree. **consensus, E-cur**
- scruttino (NC) 2025 newsletter: order as in Summary 2. Fire outfielders who can't reach 8 Power (he cuts at 9) and GKs who can't reach 10 Keeping [46480133]. One-line skill descriptions [46481086]:
  - Power: "a bit of everything".
  - Stamina: how long a player plays well.
  - Skating: low Skating means falling a lot.
  - Quickness: speed.
  - Shooting/Checking: offence and defence; "7-8 shooting score regularly".
  - PC: face-offs, "only really used by centres".
  - PI: "makes everything better".
  - Passing: "useless".

  **consensus, E-cur**
- propaans1337 2026: Power pushes opponents around, which helps both keeping and stealing the puck. Skating next (stay upright against equal Power). Stamina makes the edge fade when tired. Quickness next [46573452]. damick 2017 (linked by search): Power covers stealing (like Checking), keeping (like PC) and knocking a tackled player out of play for seconds [44027521]. **opinion, consistent with consensus**
- Hidden factors (team chemistry, loyalty boost, hidden "goal scoring") are speculation. kin_gls2 compares two players: A has better skills but scores about 50% less than B on any line or position [45848806]. nikolazpsadmin lists hidden "shot types" and "style" [45852413]. **opinion, unverified**

### Lines, stamina, ice time
- Lines change only when play stops; the line with most stamina left comes on. PP lines play when the opponent is shorthanded, BP lines when you are [44433686 kudolvol 2018]. **consensus**
- A "couple of years" before 2020 the sim was changed so players stay on ice according to their stamina [45169023 mihairo 2020-03]. **opinion (version-history hint)**
- Bug report: a 2nd line that never played and the 4th line used repeatedly in the 3rd period [45169023, 2020-03]. **opinion, E-pre**
- Low stamina lowers line average, so fewer shifts; lines can stay out 7–8 min [45772159 kin_gls2 2022]. Tired players play like fewer pucks and take fewer shots in the 3rd period [45768774 poolboy]. Contested by sohnvlbgs: a 6-stamina player was top scorer [45769016]. **contested, E-cur**
- Squads: min 10+1, recommended 20+1 [43101911]. L1–L3 + PP1–2 + BP1–2 must be filled or the match is a 0-5 walkover [46476214 floodish 2025]. 3 lines need good stamina [46400915]. A 3-line, 15-player rotation is workable [46481086]. 2 lines is a big drop [46480668]. PPs happen less often than in real hockey [46489292]. Best players go in line 1 [43101911]; one poster says put the best players at centre [43106008]. **consensus**
- No 6-man PP, no 3-man PK choice, no reserve goalie [44809438, 45784857, 45785014 mihairo]. **consensus**

## Changes to the model
1. **Positions:** model **GK, D, W, C**. LW/RW and LD/RD are mirror images (stick hand cosmetic [46569369]). No other roles exist. **consensus**
2. **Power gets the largest weight for all skaters,** more than any other skill and maybe close to double the next one [44719547 official]. Add a soft penalty below 8 and a premium for 9–10 (every cut-off post). **official + consensus**
3. **Skater "athletic core":** Stamina, Skating and Quickness come after Power for every skater, at about equal weight. Order Skating ≥ Quickness for current-era advice (scruttino) or reverse (malkin78), contested [46481086, 46489292, 44872579].
4. **Role skill:** Shooting for W (high), Checking for D (high), and both for C (medium-high each) [43101911].
5. **Puck Control:** a medium weight **only for C** (face-offs) [44384899 Crew], low elsewhere. **PI:** a small weight for all skaters, a bit more for C. **Passing:** near zero for skaters [46481086, 46544527]. **Keeping:** zero for skaters.
6. **GK:** Keeping dominant; Stamina and PI secondary; Quickness, Skating, PC and Passing small; Power, Shooting and Checking zero [46480133, 45504272].
7. **Tactic modifiers:**
   - Attacking mentality: move some D weight from Checking to Shooting + Quickness + Stamina.
   - Defensive mentality: move some W weight from Shooting to Checking + Quickness + Stamina.
   - Committed pressing: raise the Stamina weight for all skaters.
   - Passive pressing: lower the Stamina weight.
   - Source: [43154514 GPA]. **consensus-ish; no numbers anywhere**
8. **Line context:** the Stamina weight should grow as the number of lines shrinks (3 lines or fewer). A player on L1/PP is worth more than the same player on L4. Low-Stamina players are acceptable on L4 [46489292, 46480668, 45772159]. Possible input: `evaluate(player, position, tactic{mentality, pressing, nLines})`.
9. **Training-field bonus:** optionally treat the trained skill as s + 0.1·(10−s) [46257091].
10. Don't use value or total pucks as ground truth: Keeping pucks inflate skater value [45447781].
